# D1 Düzeltme Önizlemesi — UYGULANMADI (onay bekliyor)

> Yanlış-pozitifler çıkarıldı: **st-0217** (new woman = yeni komşu, doğru) ve **st-0124**
> (new man = yeni gelen, "young" değil) — bunlara DOKUNULMAYACAK.
> Aşağıdaki 7 hikaye gerçek anlamsal hata. Öneriler hazır; **onayınla** uygulanır.
> (Metinler D2 düzeltmesi sonrası güncel hâlleriyle gösteriliyor.)

---

## st-0008 — The Happy Prince ("make water" = ağlamak)
- [A2] `He made water.` → **`He cried.`**
- [A2] `"I will stay there!" he made water.` → **`"I will stay there!" he said, and cried.`**
- [A2] `"How not normal!" he made water.` → **`"How strange!" he said, and cried.`**
- ⚠️ Not: `"Around his head are light green rocks."` bu listeye takıldı ama **doğru** (gerçek "head") — dokunulmayacak.

## st-0122 — The Gift of the Magi ("head" = saç)
- [A1] `Della has a long head.` → **`Della has long hair.`**

## st-0123 — The Last Leaf ("new woman" = genç kadın)
- [A1] `Johnsy is a new woman.` → **`Johnsy is a young woman.`**
- [A2] `Johnsy is a new woman.` → **`Johnsy is a young woman.`**

## st-0126 — The Red-Headed League ("red head" = kızıl saç / kızıl saçlı)
- [A2] `Mr. Wilson is a shop man with red head.` → **`…with red hair.`**
- [A2] `The job pay well and need red head man.` → **`…need a red-haired man.`** *(ayrıca pay→pays, need→needs = D2 kalıntısı)*
- [A2] `…the red head man can live good.` → **`…the red-haired man…`**
- [B1] `Mr. Wilson is a red head man who owns a shop.` → **`Mr. Wilson is a red-haired man who owns a shop.`**
- [B1] `…a red head man to make a large book…` → **`…a red-haired man…`**
- [B2] `…with a red head.` → **`…with red hair.`** ; `red head man` → **`red-haired man`**
- Genel kural: `red head` (sıfat konumunda) → `red-haired`; `with red head` → `with red hair`.

## st-0162 — The Fox and the Stork ("long head" = uzun GAGA, hair DEĞİL)
- [A1] `The bird has a long head.` → **`The bird has a long beak.`** (leylek betimlemesi)

## st-0169 — Rapunzel ("head part" = saç; cümleler bozuk)
- [A1] `The woman move up her long head part.` → **`The woman climbed up using her long hair.`** *(move→climbed = D2)*
- [A1] `The girl bring her head part down.` → **`The girl let her long hair down.`** *(bring→let, anlam düzeltme)*

## st-0174 — The Wild Swans ("new woman")  ⚠️ BAĞLAM ONAYI GEREK
- [A1] `A new woman comes.` → Muhtemelen **`A young woman comes.`** ; ancak anlatıda "yeni biri gelir" kastediliyorsa `A new woman arrives.` de doğru olabilir. **Kararını bekliyorum.**
