### The Fox and the Grapes (B1, st-0128)

One fine day, a certain animal walked through a warm field looking for food. His inner body felt quite empty, and he really wanted something good to eat. Suddenly, he saw a beautiful collection of fine food hanging high on a green plant. The fruit looked very sweet and full of water, which made him even more ready. The animal ran toward the plant because he wanted to get those wonderful things. He jumped up with all his power, but he could not reach the fruit. If he had been tall, he might have touched them on the first try. Instead, his feet missed the branch completely, and he fell back down to the ground. Not giving up yet, the smart animal stepped back and tried again and again. He jumped as high as possible, reaching out his front feet with every single effort. However, the food remained too high above him, hanging safely out of his reach. After many hard tries, he still could not get even one single piece. Tired and angry, the animal stopped jumping and stood quietly on the ground. He was completely tired out because he had spent so much energy. He had spent that energy trying to win the award. Looking up one last time at the high plant, he realized that success was impossible.

His tired legs hurt, and his breath came fast from the difficult exercise. Slowly, the sad animal turned away from the plant and began walking down the road. To make himself feel better about failing, he told himself a little story. He spoke low that the food was probably very bad and not worth eating anyway. The animal acted like he never wanted them. He showed how people often hate what they cannot have. He had lived in the country for many years and knew how things worked. Every single morning, he went out to search for something nice. Today was no different from any other time in his long life. The sun shone brightly down upon the green fields and tall trees. Birds flew high across the blue sky while the wind blew softly. He walked past several houses and small shops on his way. Nobody was outside on the street during those quiet hours. The air felt warm and nice against his face as he moved forward. He always hoped that today would bring him a wonderful meal. Instead, he only found trouble and a high plant that troubled him.

He had already tried everything within his power to reach the top. His heart beat fast against his body from all the hard jumping. If he had longer legs, the story would have ended differently. He might have walked away with a full body and a happy mind. Now he only had empty feelings and sore feet to show for his work. The afternoon sun began to drop low behind the far hills. Dark shapes grew long across the land path where he stood alone. He took one last look at the hanging food high above him. The green leaves shook slightly in the calm evening wind. He turned his back on the plant for good and started his journey home. The long road stretched out before him in the light. He promised himself that he would find food somewhere else tomorrow. Life must go on even when things do not go the right way. He walked slowly into the night until he was completely gone from sight.

### The Ant and the Grasshopper (B1, st-0127)

All summer long, a small jumper sings and plays in the warm sun. While the jumper enjoys his game, a hard worker carries food. He carries food to store for the winter season. The worker spends every day moving bits of plant back home. He knows that cold days will arrive soon. He has worked hard because he wants to be safe. If he works all day, he will have plenty of food. He has collected many small plant pieces for his family. Every single day is important for his future safety. The warm sun is bright upon the green fields. Many small animals enjoy the beautiful weather while it lasts. The days pass quickly as summer reaches its high point. Every moment is filled with bright light and love. Nobody thinks about the cold days that will come later.

The jumper looks at the busy worker and laughs. He asks why the worker is working so hard on such a fine day. The jumper tells him that they should just sing and play instead. However, the worker keeps working and ignores the animal. The jumper does not understand why anyone would spend time like that. He believes that life is simply meant for love and song. The worker says nothing and continues moving his plant pieces. He knows that songs cannot feed anyone when snow falls. The jumper jumps around and sings another happy song. He feels completely free under the warm blue sky. Nothing can stop him from enjoying his favorite game. The bright hours pass by in a wonderful way. He has spent all his time in total peace.

Soon, the bright summer ends and the cold winter comes with snow and ice. The jumper has no food left anywhere. He is very weak and cold, and he finds himself in great trouble. Meanwhile, the worker is warm inside his house with plenty of food to eat. The white snow covers every part of the ground outside. Ice forms over the little water lines where water used to flow. The jumper shakes in the cold air because he has no home. He looks around for something to eat, but he finds nothing. His little legs feel tired and weak from the freezing wind. He wishes that he had listened to the worker before. If he had planned ahead, he would be safe now. The heavy snow continues to fall all through the long night. He realizes that his past choices were very bad.

The jumper learns an important lesson from this difficult experience. He realizes that there is a time for work and a time for play. It is good to plan for the future before hard times begin. If he had worked during the summer, he would not be weak now. He understands that fun cannot replace the need for food. The worker has shown him how to live through difficult seasons. He promises to change his ways when the next year begins. He will remember this cold winter for the rest of his life. Planning is the key to surviving the bitter ice. He closes his eyes and hopes for a better tomorrow. The bad season finally teaches him the true value of effort. He will never waste another warm summer day again. From now on, work will always come before play.

### The North Wind and the Sun (B1, st-0130)

The North Wind and the Sun had a long argument. They argued about which of them was stronger. Each character believed that his own power was greater. After talking for some time, they decided to have a test. They saw a traveler walking down a road. The traveler wore a heavy coat because the weather was cold. If the North Wind had tried, he would have blown hard. The North Wind and the Sun have talked about this for hours. The two friends agreed on a simple test. The winner would be the one who made the traveler take off his coat.

The North Wind said that he would go first. He began to blow as hard and as cold as he could. However, the harder the wind blew, the closer the man held his coat. The poor traveler felt very cold. He pulled his clothes closer to his body. The North Wind tried everything, but he could not force the man. Finally, the angry wind stopped blowing. He realized that his plan had failed completely. If the wind had succeeded, he would have won the test.

It was now time for the Sun to show his power. The Sun came out from behind the air. He began to send heat warmly upon the earth. At first, the traveler felt the nice heat. He smiled at the lovely change in the weather. As the Sun sent heat brighter, the man began to feel very warm. Soon, he could not bear the heat at all. If the Sun had not shined, the man would have kept his coat. The Sun has shown his power successfully today.

He took his coat off himself. The Sun won the argument easily. He proved that soft heat can do what force cannot. If the Sun had not tried, he would not have won. They have completed the test successfully today. The North Wind and the Sun have learned a big lesson about power.
