# Gramer Duzeltme Ornekleri (kural-tabanli, LLM yok)

Toplam 24 hikaye duzeltildi. Oncesi `-`, sonrasi `+`. SADECE gramer (ozne-yuklem/artikel), yapi+kelime korundu.

## st-0004

```diff
- She read them at school, at home, and even at dinner.
+ She reads them at school, at home, and even at dinner.
```

## st-0007

```diff
- He read old books about his house.
+ He reads old books about his house.
- He read several old books and records.
+ He reads several old books and records.
```
