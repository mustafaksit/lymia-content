## Basliklar + ozetler

- **Snow Maiden (Snegurochka)** (classic): An old couple creates a snow girl named Snegurochka and watches her grow.
- **The Straw Ox** (classic): An old couple's new strong toy attracts curious forest animals, leading to unexpected trouble.
- **The Turnip (Giant Turnip)** (classic): An old man plants a seed that grows into a huge turnip needing many helpers.
- **The Mitten** (classic): An old man loses a tiny item, drawing curious creatures into a cramped home.
- **The Enormous Cucumber / Nail Soup** (classic): A hungry man visits a poor town and claims he can make food with iron.
- **The Reluctant Dragon** (classic): A peaceful dragon loves books, but angry town people want him to leave forever.
- **The Velveteen Rabbit** (daily): A small toy rabbit hopes to become real when a young child loves him.
- **The Steadfast Tin Soldier** (classic): A one legged tin soldier watches a paper girl and faces many dangers.

### Snow Maiden (Snegurochka) (B1)

Once upon a time, an old man and an old woman lived in a small village. They had no children. This made them feel very down every single day. One morning, snow fell on the ground. The couple looked outside from the house. The man suggested that they build a lovely snow child to bring a happy feeling. They went into the yard. They shaped the ice and snow with their hands. They gave her blue eyes, a little mouth, and hair made of gold. Suddenly, the snow girl came alive with a warm breath.

The happy parents named her Snegurochka. They loved her very much. She grew up quickly. She became more beautiful day by day. When spring arrived and the sun warmed the air, Snegurochka looked down. Summer came soon. It brought hot weather and flowers across the green fields. Other young people invited Snegurochka to join them for fun in the forest. Although she felt afraid, her mother told her that going outside was good. Her mother has always supported her in every way.

If she goes to the forest, she will have fun with the group. The group walked through the trees. They sang songs and played games. The children built a small fire. They started to jump over the warm light. Snegurochka stepped near the fire. She turned into water. She has left her parents with a deep feeling of sadness. They will always remember her sweet face. Life in the village went on quietly after that day.

The old man and woman had felt by themselves before they built the snow child. They wanted a child to share their home. The little girl brought light into their lives. Every single moment with her was special. She learned how to play games with the other kids in the town. Her mother always watched her with a careful eye. The sun became very hot as the weeks passed. The warm weather changed everything around them. If the sun gives a bright light, ice will always turn into water. That scientific fact became clear when Snegurochka got close to the fire.

The group of children cried when she turned into water. They ran back to the house to tell the parents. The mother and father came out quickly. They found only a small amount of water on the ground. Their hearts were full of heavy pain. They have kept her memory alive in their minds. The village remained quiet as time went on. People still talk about the wonderful snow girl. If anyone visits the village, they can hear the story. The old couple never forgot the sweet child they had loved so much.

### The Straw Ox (B1)

Once upon a time, an old man and an old woman lived together. They lived in a small house near a deep forest. The man built a wonderful thing for the woman because she wanted something special. They put liquid and land material over the structure so that it would be strong. They made it look like a farm animal to please her every single day. The man worked hard on the project for a long time. He used wood and other materials to make the body look right. The woman felt very happy when she saw the finished work. She thanked the man and gave him a warm hug. They kept the structure inside their home during the cold winter nights. They talked about how much they loved each other while sitting by the fire. Life was quiet and good for the old couple in their small home.

One fine morning, the woman took the structure out to the green field. She put it near the tall trees where the grass grew tall and green. She left it there while she worked on other things around the farm. She went back to the house to clean the rooms and prepare food. Forest animals came out from the dark woods because they felt interested in the new object. A big bear walked toward the structure and asked who it was. When the structure did not answer, the bear became angry. The bear tried to hit the structure with his hand. However, the hand of the bear stuck fast to the surface on the structure. The bear pulled and pulled, but he could not move away at all. He stayed right there because he was completely stuck to the side.

Soon after that, a gray animal came along and saw the bear stuck to the structure. The animal asked what was happening and tried to help his friend. He pulled the bear by the body to move him away from the field. Instead, the gray animal also stuck to the structure and could not move. He tried everything he knew, but his hands stayed completely fixed in place. Both creatures stood together and looked at each other with fear in their eyes. They could not understand why this strange thing had happened to them. The sun shone down upon the green field as they waited for help. Hours passed by, and the afternoon sun became very hot. The two animals felt tired and wished they had never walked near the structure.

Next, another animal arrived and tried to speak with them. This new visitor was small and fast, and he thought he was very smart. He looked at the bear and the gray animal with a funny smile on his face. He asked why they were standing so close together in the open field. Then he touched the surface to see what it was made of. Just like the others, he also touched the liquid and could not pull his hand away. Thus, the structure caught several forest animals without any trouble at all. All three creatures were stuck together and could not escape. They made loud noises, but no one else was around to hear them. The sun began to go down, and the evening air turned cold.

The old man and the old woman came outside to check on the field. They were very happy to find those animals there. They smiled when they saw the bear, the gray animal, and the smart visitor. The man walked over and spoke to the animals in a calm voice. He told them that they should not touch things that did not belong to them. The woman agreed with the man and nodded her head slowly. They took the animals home and kept them safe inside a nice building. They gave the animals food and water to help them feel better. Every forest animal learned a good lesson about staying away from strange things in the field. From that day on, the old couple lived a good life with their new friends.

### The Steadfast Tin Soldier (B1)

Once upon a time, a group of metal figures was made in a factory. The man had only enough metal for twenty-five figures. Every single soldier stood straight on the table. The last one had only one leg. Although he had just one leg, he stood as firmly as the others. On the table, there was a paper house and a lovely paper dancer. The dancer stood on one leg while she raised her arms high. The metal soldier thought that she was just like him. He watched her all day because he wanted to make her his wife. When night came, a black toy character became angry with the soldier. The character told him to look away from the dancer. The soldier acted like he did not hear him at all. The next morning, the poor soldier fell down from the window straight onto the street below.

Two young boys found him outside in the rain. They placed him into a small paper boat. The boat moved down a dark water path where a big animal demanded a ticket. Because the soldier had no ticket, the boat moved into a wide area of water. The water covered him completely. Suddenly, a large fish swam up and took the soldier whole. After some time, the fish was caught and bought by a cook. When the cook cut the fish open, she found the soldier inside. She carried him back to the same room. The soldier was very happy to see his lovely paper dancer again. Suddenly, a small child picked up the soldier and threw him into the warm fire. A strong wind blew the paper dancer into the fire too. They burned together until nothing remained except a small heart made of metal.

Many days have passed since the fire burned the little metal soldier. If someone looks closely at the ashes, they will see that small metal heart. The metal heart has stayed there for a long time. People have talked about the brave soldier and the paper dancer. Their story has lived on in this room. The small house and the table are still part of the room. Children have played games near the window where the soldier fell. No one has forgotten the little toy with one leg. Every afternoon, the light shines across the floor. The memory of the dancer and the soldier remains strong. We have remembered their love through all these years.
