## Basliklar + ozetler

- **The Emperor's New Clothes** (classic): Two clever men sell a foolish ruler magical new clothes that nobody can see.
- **The Elves and the Shoemaker** (classic): Poor shoemakers find mystery help at night and try to discover who makes shoes.
- **How the Camel Got His Hump** (classic): A lazy worker refuses to help, so a big man gives him a special change.
- **Rip Van Winkle** (classic): Rip walks into the forest, drinks magic water, and sleeps for twenty long years.
- **Puss in Boots** (classic): A sad young person gets a smart animal that asks for clothes and food.
- **The Three Questions** (classic): A powerful man asks three hard questions and visits a wise old person for help.
- **The Oak and the Reeds** (classic): A strong big plant and small weak plants face a powerful wind together.
- **The Frogs Who Wished for a King** (classic): Some water animals want a new leader and ask high powers for help.

## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### The Emperor's New Clothes (B1)

Long ago, a king cared about nothing except fine clothes. His whole life was spent trying on new items every single day. One fine morning, two men visited the town. They claimed to be master workers. They told the king that they could make the most wonderful clothes in the whole world. The men explained that the material had a special power. It would remain completely hidden to anyone who was bad or wrong for their job. The king thought this was a good idea. He could easily find out which leaders were poor at their work. He gave the men a lot of money to start working immediately. The king has always loved fine clothes. If the king wears the clothes, he will see who is nice. He has never seen such wonderful material before today. The king has spent much money on nothing. He wants to find out the truth about his workers.

The two men set up empty tables. They made movements to look like they worked very hard. Soon, the king sent his old minister to see how the product was coming along. When the old man looked at the empty room, he saw nothing at all. He was too afraid to admit it. He did not want anyone to think he was bad or wrong for his position. Soon after, the king visited the room himself with other people. They also saw nothing on the tables. Yet they all spoke well of the wonderful colors and patterns. Everyone agreed that the clothes were great. They told the king they looked good. The old minister has visited the room already. If the minister looks closely, he will see nothing. He has not told the truth about the empty room. The minister has kept the secret from the king.

The king felt very glad. He decided to wear his new suit during the big town street show. On the day of the show, the king walked through the streets wearing his hidden suit. All the people standing along the road made sounds of joy. They looked at him and spoke well of his fine new clothes. Nobody wanted to look bad or unusual in front of their neighbors. Suddenly, a small child cried out that the king was wearing nothing at all. The true words spread quickly through the crowd until everyone was talking about the secret. The red-faced king heard them and finally realized that other people had missed the truth. He knew the child was right. He kept walking proudly until the end of the long street. The small child has shouted out the truth. If the king finishes the walk, everyone will know the secret. The king has walked through the long street. He will remember this strange day for a long time.

### Rip Van Winkle (B1)

Rip Van Winkle was a friendly man who lived near high mountains. He did not like hard work on his farm. His wife was often very angry about this. Rip would go into the forest with his dog and gun. He enjoyed walking through the green trees and looking at nature. He spent many hours there because he loved the quiet place. If he went into the woods, his dog always followed him closely. Rip had never liked to work hard on the family property. He preferred to look at the sky and the tall trees. His wife told him that he did nothing useful every single day. She would get very angry when he came home late. Rip always tried to find new ways to avoid her big voice.

One afternoon, Rip walked higher into the mountains. He heard someone call his name from deep among the rocks. He saw a strange little man carrying a heavy drink. The man made signs for Rip to follow him. Rip followed the stranger to a dark place. A group of odd people played a game there. They were very quiet and only made sound with heavy balls. When they saw Rip, they stopped and stared. They gave some drink and told Rip to taste it. Rip had taken several drinks because he felt very dry. Soon he felt tired and fell into a very deep sleep. When he woke up, the sun was looking bright. His gun was old, and his beard was long and white. His faithful dog was missing from the area. Rip had slept for a very long time indeed. He could not understand what had happened to his favorite gun. The little men had disappeared into the dark mountain places. Rip moved his hand over his eyes because he felt completely confused. He stood up and looked around the strange place.

Rip stood up slowly and walked down the mountain. He reached his village, but did not recognize any houses. Children laughed at his long beard and old clothes. He went to his house, but it was empty. Rip tried to talk to people, but they spoke differently. An old woman looked at him and recognized his voice. A neighbor told the crowd that twenty years had passed. The people listened closely to his history. Rip had missed two full decades of his normal life. His wife had died many years before he returned home. His grown daughter welcomed him into her own house. The local residents gathered around to hear his strange report. They could not believe that a man had slept for so long. Rip told them about the strange little men in the hills.

Rip spent the rest of his life as a famous speaker. Visitors came to listen to his story about the little men. He lived a calm life with his family and friends. People from all around the country visited his new home. They wanted to hear the amazing report about the mountains. Rip always answered their questions with a big smile. He had become a well known person in the local town. His young family members loved to sit near him every evening. If anyone asked about the mountains, Rip shared his memories. He never forgot the hidden game played by the odd men. His quiet days passed quickly among people who cared for him. He finally found happiness after all those years of sleep.

### The Three Questions (B1)

Once upon a time, a powerful king wanted to know three important things. He wanted to know the right time to begin each action. He wanted to know who the most important people were. He wanted to know what the most important task was. Because no one could give him a good answer, he decided to visit an old man. The old man lived in a garden near the forest. Many people came to see him for advice. The king dressed in plain clothes and walked alone until he reached the garden. When the king asked his questions, the old man kept working without speaking. Seeing that the old man was tired, the king took the tool and continued working. Hours passed while the king worked, and the sun began to go down. Suddenly, a bad stranger came out of the woods with a hurt arm. The man was losing blood fast. The king rushed over, cleaned the wound, and covered it carefully. Tired from the hard labor, the king fell into bed on the floor. When he woke up, the stranger looked at him with deep sadness. The man explained that he was an enemy who had come to kill the king. The king was glad to make peace and promised to give back all lands. Before leaving, the king asked the old man one final time for the answers. The old man explained that the most important time is now. The person you are with is the most important person. The good thing to do is to help that person.

The king thought about these words for a long time. He understood that life is short and very valuable. Every day brings new chances to do good things for others. If you help someone today, you will feel happy. The king had learned a great lesson from the old man. He walked back to his big house with a calm mind. His heart was full of love for all people. From that day on, the king tried to live a better life. He always made sure to help anyone in need. If a problem came up, he stayed calm and thought carefully. He never forgot the important lesson he learned in the garden. The country grew strong and calm under his rule. People from far away came to hear his wonderful stories. They wanted to know how he became such a good leader. The king always told them about the old man in the garden. He told them that the present moment is all we really have. We must use our time well and always be kind to others. This is the secret to a happy and good life.

Many years passed after that special day in the garden. The king ruled his land with great care and constant attention. If anyone had a serious problem, the king listened to them directly. He walked among the citizens to see their daily lives. Children played in the streets while adults worked in shops. No one suffered from hunger because the king shared food freely. The army stayed strong, but peace remained the main goal. Visitors from foreign countries arrived to study the system. They could not believe how well the community operated. The king always shared his personal story with the guests. He told every new generation about the wise old man. If you remember to live in the present, you will find peace. The future depends on what we do right now. Therefore, the king kept his heart open to every person. He ensured that kindness guided every single action he took. His name became a symbol of hope and proper leadership. Even when difficult challenges appeared, he remained completely steady. The country thrived and remained secure through all seasons. Every single citizen felt safe and deeply respected. The king smiled as he watched his people live well. He knew that helping others brought true satisfaction. His final years were filled with quiet strength and dignity. When the king finally passed away, the whole nation mourned. They remembered his fair laws and his gentle nature. Statues were built to honor his memory in the capital. Children learned about his wonderful rule in school. The lesson of the garden lived on through the years. People understood that the present moment is truly everything. If we treat each other well, the world improves. This simple truth remained the greatest guide for everyone.
