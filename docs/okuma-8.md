# Okuma Raporu 8

Bu partide 8 hikaye tamamlandi.

## Basliklar + ozetler

- **The Fisherman and the Genie** (adventure): A poor man finds an old container in the water and opens it.
- **Momotaro, the Peach Boy** (classic): Brave Momotaro sets out with animal friends to defeat distant bad people.
- **Urashima Taro** (classic): Taro rescues a turtle, follows it underwater to a wondrous palace, receiving a secret box.
- **The Grateful Crane (Tsuru no Ongaeshi)** (classic): An old man saves a trapped crane, then a mysterious woman arrives and changes everything.
- **Issun-boshi (One-Inch Boy)** (classic): A tiny boy leaves his village to help a child in a distant city.
- **Anansi and the Pot of Wisdom** (classic): Anansi tries to hide a magical pot of wisdom, but a clever child offers help.
- **The Tiger, the Brahmin and the Jackal** (classic): A kind man finds a trapped beast in a box and must decide how to help.
- **The Blue Jackal** (classic): A hungry man falls into bright blue dye and becomes the king of animals.

## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### Anansi and the Pot of Wisdom (B1)

Long ago, Anansi decided to collect all information of the world. He believed that he would become the most important person. The sky god gave him a big container with this information. Anansi wanted to keep the container safe from everyone else. He decided to hide the container high up in a tall green tree. To bring the heavy container up, he tied a long line around it. He worked hard to move the container off the ground. He wanted to protect the special knowledge from any person. Every single day, Anansi tried to make his life much better. He thought that having all knowledge would give him power. No other animal in the world had such great knowledge. Anansi wanted to keep every single piece of information hidden. He did not want anyone else to share this great gift. The sky god watched him from above with a serious face. Anansi had planned to climb the tall tree very quickly.

Carrying the container in front made climbing very difficult. He could not reach the branches because the big container blocked his way. His young son watched him struggle with the strange task from below. The boy asked why his father did not tie the container to his back instead. Anansi realized that the bright child was right. He felt surprised because he thought he already knew everything. Anansi had never considered another way to carry the heavy container. He looked down at his young son with an unusual expression. The boy spoke again to explain his simple idea clearly. Anansi listened to the child and thought about the suggestion. If he had tied the container to his back, climbing would have been easy. He felt quite silly because a little child found the answer. His mind suffered a lot during this strange moment. Anansi had spent many hours trying to solve the climbing problem. Now he understood that even a young boy can be very smart.

In his angry state, the animal dropped the container down to the ground. The container broke into small pieces upon the earth. All information flew out into the wind and spread across the world. Since that day, anyone can find a little bit of knowledge anywhere. If he had listened to his son, he would have saved the container. Anansi has learned a lot from this strange experience today. The broken container could never be put back together again. Knowledge went into every corner of the whole world. People now read books and learn new things every single day. If Anansi had been patient, the story would have ended differently. He lost all the information that the sky god had given him. The wonderful gift of knowledge now belongs to every person. Anansi will always remember this terrible mistake for the rest of his life. He has found out that listening to others is always a good choice. Today, knowledge remains free for everyone because of that accident.

### The Grateful Crane (Tsuru no Ongaeshi) (B1)

Once upon a time, an old man walked through a cold forest.

He saw a white bird that was caught by a bad trap.

The poor animal could not move because its body hurt.

Feeling deep trouble, the man carefully set the bird free.

If he helps the bird, he will feel very happy today.

The bird has opened its big body and flown into the sky.

It looked down at the old man with grateful eyes.

Later that evening, a lovely young woman arrived at his house.

She explained that she had lost her way in the snow.

The kind man and his wife welcomed the stranger inside.

The girl soon became like a daughter to them.

One day, she told them that she wanted to produce material.

She entered a small room and asked them never to watch.

Strange and wonderful sounds came from behind the door.

When she finally emerged, she gave them a piece of material.

The material looked like snow.

The old man took the material to the town market.

A rich buyer paid a lot of money for it.

However, the couple grew very interested about the work.

The old woman opened the door just a little bit.

Inside the room, a thin white bird was producing material.

The bird noticed them and realized its secret was known.

The bird flew out the window into the air.

It changed back into the lovely young woman.

She spoke with trouble, explaining she was the bird he saved.

Because her identity was discovered, she could not stay.

She thanked them, spread her white body, and flew away.

The old man and his wife always remembered the kind bird.

They lived quietly in their small house for many years.

Sometimes they looked up at the sky and thought about her.

They wondered if she was flying far away from danger.

The winter snow came back many times over the years.

Each time, the old couple stayed warm inside their home.

They never forgot the strange and wonderful visitor they met.

Her memory stayed in their hearts for a long time.

Life went on peacefully for the old man and his wife.

They always shared stories about the lovely young woman.

Their friends in town often asked about the white material.

The couple only smiled and kept the old secret safe.

They knew that some things in life are quite magical.

Every morning, the sun rose over the quiet town.

The old man walked outside to check the cold weather.

He always looked toward the sky with a gentle smile.

He hoped that the white bird was safe and happy.

His wife usually joined him near the front door.

They stood together and watched the morning light shine.

Even without the magical material, they felt truly rich.

Their simple life together was full of quiet peace.

They had loved the bird like a real child.

That special memory lived forever in their minds.

The story of the bird became a local legend.

People in the town talked about it during cold nights.

No one ever saw the lovely young woman again.

Yet the old couple always remembered her tender face.

They knew she had been a very special friend.

Time passed slowly as the years went by.

The old man and his wife grew older together.

They always took care of each other every single day.

Their home remained full of warmth and love.

They never stopped believing in the magic of that winter.

Sometimes the wind blew softly against their window.

It sounded just like the wings of a white bird.

They looked at each other and smiled in silence.

They knew the world was full of wonderful surprises.

That one moment in the forest changed their lives forever.

They had done a good deed on a cold day.

The reward they received was a daughter's love.

Even though she had to leave, she left a lasting mark.

Their hearts remained connected to the beautiful white bird.

Every part of their story stayed fresh in their minds.

They lived out their days in peace and happiness.

This is the end of the wonderful winter story.

### Urashima Taro (B1)

Taro was a young man who lived in a small village near the sea. Every day, he took his boat out to catch fish for his family. One afternoon, he saw a group of children making trouble for a small animal. Taro felt sorry for the animal and asked the kids to leave it alone. The kind man picked up the animal and put it back into the water. The animal swam away, but it looked back at Taro with a happy expression. The next day, the same animal appeared and invited him to visit a water place. Taro was surprised, but he climbed onto the back of the large animal. They went down through the water until they reached a house of gold and white stone. A beautiful woman came out to welcome him because she was the animal. She thanked him and offered him fine food and music inside the room. Taro stayed in the place for a few days and enjoyed every moment. However, he soon began to feel sad for his mother and his village. He told the woman that he must return home, though he was sad to leave. The woman gave him a heavy box, but she told him never to open it.

Taro rode back on the animal until he reached the familiar beach. When he stepped onto the land, he looked around because everything had changed. The houses were different, the people were new, and nobody knew his name. He realized that hundreds of years had passed while he was in the water place. Feeling very sad, the old man forgot the warning and opened the box. A white mass of smoke came out of the container up toward the sky. Taro felt his body grow old and weak in a single second. He fell onto the ground, finally returning to the past of history. If he had not opened the box, he would have stayed young. He has learned a hard lesson about the passage of time in this world. Many years have passed, and his family is gone. He will always remember the beautiful woman and the wonderful place under the sea. Taro will always keep these memories in his mind. The water place was very quiet and calm during his stay. He has understood that time moves fast when people are happy.

Life in the village continued without him through all those long years. The people who lived there now did not know his face or his name. He walked slowly along the beach where he had played as a boy. The sun came up brightly upon the water, but nothing looked familiar to him now. He sat down on a stone and thought about his mother and his old life. The heavy box remained in his hand as a reminder of his mistake. If he could change the past, he would make a different choice. He has accepted his end and the final stop of his long journey. The story of Taro remains an important lesson for everyone in the village. Children still listen to the old story when they gather near the water. They learn that being good and having time are valuable things in this life. Taro has found his final rest by the edge of the blue water. The waves wash softly against the land day after day. His name lives on in the history of the small town. Nobody will ever forget the man who traveled to the big house under the sea.
