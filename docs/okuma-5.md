# Okuma Raporu 5

Bu partide 8 hikaye tamamlandi.

## Basliklar + ozetler

- **Rapunzel** (classic): A lonely girl lives high in a tower until a stranger hears her call.
- **The Star Money** (classic): A kind girl with no money gives her clothes away and meets falling stars.
- **Thumbelina** (classic): A tiny girl named Thumbelina embarks on a brave adventure after a toad captures her.
- **The Princess and the Pea** (classic): A prince searches worldwide for a true princess and plans an unusual test.
- **The Nightingale** (classic): A rich man keeps a singing bird until a strange metal bird arrives.
- **The Wild Swans** (classic): A girl must craft magical items to free her brothers turned into swans.
- **The Fir Tree** (classic): A small tree longs to grow tall while children and workers decide its fate.
- **Cinderella** (classic): A kind girl enslaved by her cruel family gets a magical dress for a ball.

## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### Cinderella (B1)

Once upon a time, a kind girl named Cinderella lived in a big house with her family.

After her father died, her mother friend and her new sisters treated her very badly.

She had to work all day while they rested.

She worked from morning until night.

She cleaned the rooms and prepared food.

She never had any rest.

She always felt sad and alone in that big house.

Her life was very difficult every single day.

She wanted to be happy and free from the hard work.

She often thought about a better future.

She hoped that things would change soon.

She dreamed of a different life.

She wanted to see the world outside her home.

One day, a written note for a big social gathering arrived from the king.

The family prepared nice clothes and left Cinderella alone at home.

She felt very sad because she wanted to go too.

She sat down and cried in the kitchen.

She looked at her old and bad clothes.

She knew that she could not go to the gathering like that.

She wished for a good event to happen.

She wanted to dance and wear beautiful clothes.

She wanted to meet new people at the gathering.

She hoped that someone would help her.

She stayed near the cold area and thought about the music and lights.

She imagined how wonderful it would be to attend.

Suddenly, an unusual woman appeared and helped Cinderella get ready for the gathering.

The woman made a wonderful dress and special shoes for her.

Cinderella was told that she must return home before twelve in the night.

The woman used her power to create beautiful things.

Cinderella put on the new dress and the lovely shoes.

She looked in the glass and could not believe her eyes.

She looked like a young lady from a book.

The woman told her to enjoy the night.

She also reminded her about the time.

Cinderella promised to come back before the clock struck twelve.

She thanked the woman for the wonderful help.

She left the house with a happy heart.

At the gathering, the king son saw Cinderella and danced with her all evening.

She was so happy that she forgot about the time.

When the clock began to strike twelve, she ran away quickly.

She left the room and ran down the stone path.

The young man followed her, but he could not catch her.

While she was running down the floor, one glass shoe fell off her foot.

She did not stop to pick it up because she was too afraid.

She ran out into the dark night.

She lost her shoe on the ground.

She returned home in her old clothes before anyone noticed.

She remembered the wonderful dance with the young man with a smile on her face.

The young man found the shoe.

He decided to look for the girl who could wear it.

He visited every house in the town until he came to her home.

He wanted to find the strange guest from the gathering.

He carried the glass shoe with him everywhere.

He asked every young woman to try on the shoe.

Many girls tried, but the shoe did not fit any of them.

The young man came to Cinderella house at last.

Her family tried to hide her from the young man.

They wanted one of her sisters to marry the young man instead.

However, the young man insisted on seeing every person in the house.

Cinderella came forward and asked to try the shoe.

She showed the other glass shoe to the young man.

It fit her foot perfectly, which proved that she was the guest from the gathering.

The young man was very happy, and they lived a good life together.

He recognized her face immediately.

He knew that she was the special girl from that night.

They got married soon after that day.

Her family could no longer treat her badly.

Cinderella left the big house and went to live in the large building.

She never had to do hard work again.

She and the young man enjoyed a wonderful life together.

Everyone in the country celebrated their love.

They were happy for the rest of their days.

### Thumbelina (B1)

Once upon a time, a good woman wanted a child very much. She did not know how to get one. She went to see an old woman. This person gave her a little seed to plant in the ground. After the seed grew into a large, beautiful plant, it opened up. A tiny girl was inside. Because she was no bigger than a finger, the woman called her Thumbelina. Thumbelina had a nice bed made from a dry fruit skin. She spent her days singing sweet songs. One night, a bad animal jumped through the window. It carried the little girl away into the garden. The strange animal wanted Thumbelina to marry her son. She left the poor child alone on a green plant part in the water. Thumbelina began to cry because she did not want to live there. Some fish in the water felt very sorry for her. They helped pull the plant part away from the land.

Then a large bug flew down. It caught the girl and carried her high up into a tree. However, the other bugs did not like how Thumbelina looked. The first bug left her alone in the forest. The little girl lived through the warm summer by herself. She ate sweet food from plants and drank water from other parts. When cold winter came, the weather grew very bad. Thumbelina had to walk through the snow. She found a small home under a tree where a kind field mouse lived. The mouse gave her food and a warm bed. Soon, she told her that a rich animal wanted to marry her. Thumbelina did not like this animal because he lived down below. He never saw the sun during his life. During her stay there, she found a bird that seemed dead upon the cold ground.

Thumbelina took care of the bird. She brought it back to life with her love. When spring came again, the bird wanted to fly away to a warm country. He asked the girl to go with him. Thumbelina climbed upon the bird, and they flew far away across the sea. The bird brought her to a wonderful land full of lovely plants and green trees. There, a tiny boy lived inside the most beautiful plant in the garden. When he saw Thumbelina, he fell in love with her. He asked her to marry him. She finally found her true home. They lived happily together for all time.

### The Wild Swans (B1)

Once upon a time, a king lived in a great house with his dear children. The older children were eleven brothers. The youngest was a sweet little girl named Kizkardes. They were very happy until a bad new head came into their lives. The woman did not love the children. She sent the young girl away to a farm. The bad head also changed the eleven brothers into wild birds with a dark spell. The big birds flew far away across the water into another country. When Kizkardes grew older, she returned to the house to find her family. She walked through a deep forest until she saw the beautiful birds near the sea. The birds turned back into men when the sun went down behind the mountains. They told her about the dark spell which could only be broken by a great work. Kizkardes had to make eleven shirts from green plants that grew near the dark holes. She could never speak a single word until every shirt was completely finished. The strong girl began to pick the green leaves and work day and night without rest.

She sat in a quiet place and made the clothes with her own hands. A young king found her during a hunting trip. He brought her to his big house as his wife. Even though she never spoke to him, the king loved her nice face very much. Some bad people in the house told the king that his wife was a bad person. They led her to a high public square because she would not answer their questions. While the people watched from below, the strong girl held the green shirts in her hands. Eleven wild birds flew down from the sky and stood around her on the ground. Kizkardes threw the green shirts over the big birds just in time. The spell broke, and the eleven brothers stood up as healthy young men again. The youngest brother still had one bird part because his shirt lacked a hand cover. The woman's dark plan failed completely. The family lived happily ever after. If she had not finished the work, the spell would have stayed forever. Now the brothers have returned to their home. They have lived together in peace since that day. The king has understood the truth about his dear wife. They have built a wonderful life in the great house. Every person in the country has known their story well. The young girl has shown great strength and love for her family.
