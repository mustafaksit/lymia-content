### The Tale of Benjamin Bunny (B1)

Once upon a time, little Benjamin Bunny and his friend Peter Rabbit went for a long walk. They walked near a big garden. Peter had trouble there before, so he felt very careful. Benjamin, however, was quite strong in his mind. He wanted to see the food house again. They walked quietly through the field until they reached a high wall.

Benjamin easily jumped down, while Peter stayed on top. He remembered his past mistake. Suddenly, a large cat appeared near them. This made both family members very afraid. The fast animals quickly hid under a large bag. They stayed safe from the cat. Unfortunately, the bag fell completely over them. They could not move out at all.

They sat together in the dark and wondered how they would return home. They have wondered for some time. If they stay longer, they will have more trouble. After some time, old Mr. Bunny arrived with a big stick. He brought his son and younger relative back. He was quite angry because they had gone into the bad place. He took them both home and gave them a little task as a lesson.

They have learned a hard lesson today. If they go out again, they will stay careful. Peter has understood the big danger. Benjamin has understood the rule too. They will never go near that place again. Old Mr. Bunny watched them closely because he wanted to keep them safe. The evening came very quickly. They sat inside the warm house and ate some good food together. Peace returned to the little family.

Life went on for the animals. They played games in the green field. Days passed by very slowly. The light appeared brightly every morning. They always remembered the big cat and the high wall. Fear kept them away from danger. They have grown much older now. Every single member felt happy. They lived safely through the year.

Sometimes they talked about that story. Peter usually shook his head, and Benjamin just smiled at him. They knew they had been lucky. Luck saved them from the cat. They promised to follow their father. Good behavior brought peace to their home. That story stayed in their minds. They never forgot the big lesson. Everything was good at the end.

### Sleeping Beauty (Brier Rose) (B1)

Once upon a time, a king and his family lived in a very big house. They had a baby girl and invited many people to a great party. Several good women came to give wonderful gifts to the child. They brought sweet things and nice items for the young baby. The king wanted to make everyone feel very welcome and glad. The big rooms were full of music, food, and smiling talk. Everyone talked about how lovely the little girl was. The party continued for many hours into the evening. People danced and sang songs to celebrate the happy birth. The mother and father were proud of their child. They gave food and drink to every single guest. The event went on until the night was dark. Every person in the house had a wonderful time. They wanted the good life to continue without end.

However, one old woman was not invited. This made her very angry. She came into the room and told everyone a bad fact. She said that the young girl would stop living upon a point. One good woman changed this rule so that the child would rest. The child would rest instead of passing away during those years. The king tried to keep his daughter safe by burning items. He burned every sharp tool in the country. When the girl grew, she walked around the house. She found a small room up top. An old person was working there with a sharp tool. The girl touched the sharp point immediately. As soon as she touched it, she fell down. She fell into a deep rest upon a bed.

Everyone else in the house also fell down during that same moment. Even the horses stopped moving. The birds stopped moving too. A large wall of green plants grew around the building. Many years passed by. A long time went without anyone entering the house. Different men tried to visit the place. The green plants were too strong for them. Finally, a young man came to the area. He came after hearing the story about the resting beauty. When he reached the place, the plants opened up. The plants opened to let him through. He walked inside the quiet rooms. He found the beautiful girl resting on her bed. He gave her a kiss. The kiss woke her up from her long rest.

Everyone else in the house woke up too. They were very happy. The man and the girl lived a wonderful life together. They lived together from that day on. The sun was bright upon the big house. The sun was bright upon the green land around it. People came from far and wide to see the happy family. They brought flowers and gifts to show their love. They brought support for the king and his wife. The king and his wife thanked everyone for kind words. Peace filled the whole country for many years. Every person in the land remembered the story. They remembered the story of the resting beauty.

### Beauty and the Beast (B1)

Once upon a time, a rich man lived in a big town with his lovely children. Bad business hit the family. They lost all money. They moved into a small house far away. The sisters complained about their new life. The youngest daughter named Beauty worked hard. She always smiled at everyone in the house.

One day, the father heard good news. A ship with his goods had finally arrived at the edge. Before he left, he asked his children a question. He asked what kind of present they wanted him to bring back. The older sisters requested expensive clothes and gold. Beauty only asked for a simple red flower.

The man reached the place. He found that his goods were gone. He had nothing left. During his dark and cold journey home through the forest, he lost his way. He walked in deep snow. Suddenly, he saw a large house. It was bright with lights. The table was full of food.

The man entered the warm building. He ate a nice meal. He slept in a comfortable bed. The next morning, as he walked outside, he saw a beautiful garden. He picked a red flower for Beauty. Immediately, a terrible animal appeared. The animal shouted that he would kill the man for stealing property.

The worried father explained the situation. He said the flower was only a small gift for his youngest daughter. The big animal agreed to let him go. He said one of his daughters must come to live in the house. The man returned home. Beauty decided to give herself up. She traveled to the strange house.

Although the animal looked bad, he treated Beauty with great care. He gave her a lovely room. Every evening they talked together. Beauty began to feel deep love for the strange animal. However, she saw in a special glass that her father was sick. She asked to visit him.

The animal allowed her to go home for a short time. She had to promise that she would return soon. Her family was extremely happy to see her. Her stay became too long because she enjoyed being with them. Suddenly, she remembered the animal. She rushed back to the house because she was afraid that he was dead.

She found the poor animal resting still in the garden. She cried while she held him closely. Beauty told the animal that she loved him. Her love broke the dark spell. The animal immediately changed into a fine man. They lived happily together for all time.
