### Silver Blaze (B1)

In the south of England, there is a small town called King’s Pyland. People in the town love animals very much.

The best animal is a horse named Silver Blaze. He runs very fast and is very strong. His coat is bright and looks good. His owner is Colonel Ross.

Colonel Ross hopes Silver Blaze will win the big race soon. One evening, a week before the race, Colonel Ross goes to the stable. He wants to check on Silver Blaze. The stable is quiet, and the door is locked.

But something does not feel right. The stable boy, Hunter, is inside. He does not answer when the Colonel calls. The Colonel opens the door and finds Hunter on the floor.

Hunter is hurt and does not move. Colonel Ross sends for the police right away. The police look for Silver Blaze everywhere. They cannot find him anywhere.

The stable is empty. No one in the town has seen the horse that night. Colonel Ross is very worried. He decides to ask a well-known man for help.

The next morning, Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson arrive at King’s Pyland. Holmes looks at the stable and the ground near it. He sees strange marks by the door. He finds a few red drops on the ground.

He also sees a small piece of cloth on a small plant. Holmes asks many questions about the people who work with Silver Blaze. A man named Fitzroy Simpson visited the stable the night before. Simpson asked many questions about Silver Blaze.

He even offered Hunter money to tell him things. Hunter said no, but later he felt very tired. Holmes also talks to Silas Brown. Brown trains horses at a farm near King’s Pyland.

Brown is not easy to talk to. He had argued with Colonel Ross before. Holmes sees that Brown’s shoes have the same strange marks as near the stable. Brown tries to explain, but Holmes does not believe him.

The next day, Holmes and Watson walk near the farm. They see a group of horses in a field. One horse looks like Silver Blaze, but it is not the same color. Holmes smiles and says, 'I think we have found him, Watson.'

Someone has put paint on the horse to change his look. But Holmes knows it is Silver Blaze. Holmes explains that Silas Brown took the horse. Brown wanted to stop Silver Blaze from running in the race.

Colonel Ross is very happy. Silver Blaze is safe just in time for the race. On the day of the big race, Silver Blaze runs faster than ever. He wins the race easily.

Everyone is happy for the strong horse. Holmes and Watson leave King’s Pyland. They are happy they solved the problem. As they walk away, Holmes says, 'The most important thing was what did not happen.'

Watson asks what he means, but Holmes does not explain. The next day, Watson writes about the case. He thinks about the strange marks and the red drops. He remembers how Holmes found the piece of cloth.

Watson knows Holmes is very good at solving problems. He hopes to learn more from him in the future. The case of Silver Blaze shows how smart Holmes is. It also shows how much people in the town care about the horse.

The race was very important to everyone. Now, the town can celebrate the victory together. People will remember this day for a long time. They are glad Silver Blaze is safe and back home.

### A Scandal in Bohemia (B1)

In the year eighteen eighty-eight, a man named Sherlock Holmes lived in London. He was very good at solving hard problems. Many people came to his house to ask for help.

One evening, a man arrived at Holmes’s door. He did not say his real name. He said his name was Count Kramm. But Holmes knew this was not true.

The man took off his coat and hat. Holmes saw he was really a king. The king came from a country far away. He had a big problem.

The king would marry a woman soon. But a lady named Irene Adler had a photograph. The photograph showed the king and Irene together. She wanted money or she would send the photograph.

The king was afraid. The photograph could cause trouble for his marriage. He asked Holmes to get the photograph back. The wedding was in three days.

Holmes agreed to help. He put on old clothes. He looked like a poor worker. He went to Irene Adler’s street to learn about her.

Holmes met some of her friends. They said she was kind and smart. The next day, Holmes dressed as a man who worked with horses. He waited near her house.

Holmes saw a man named Godfrey Norton run out. Norton called a coach. He asked Irene to come with him to a church. Holmes followed them.

Holmes acted like he would help at their wedding. After the wedding, Irene and Norton went home. Holmes returned to his own house.

Holmes made a plan with his friend Watson. They would go to Irene’s house the next evening. Holmes knew she kept the photograph in her sitting room. He asked Watson to throw something that made smoke and shout 'fire.'

When Irene heard 'fire,' she ran to save her photograph. Holmes would see where she hid it. At first, everything went as they planned. Irene pulled the photograph from a part of the wall.

But then she looked at Holmes and smiled. She said, 'I know who you are, Mr. Holmes.' Holmes was very surprised. No one had ever done this to him before.

The next morning, Holmes and the king went to Irene’s house. They wanted to take the photograph. But the house was empty. Irene and her new husband had left the country.

Irene left a letter for Holmes. She said she would not cause trouble for the king. She kept the photograph to keep herself safe. The king was happy and thanked Holmes.

But Holmes said Irene Adler was the only woman who did better than him. He kept a photograph of her in his house. He always spoke of her with respect. The case was over, but Holmes never forgot her.

If anyone asks Holmes about this case, he will tell the story again. He has never met a woman like Irene since then. She made a big mark on his life. He thinks she was the smartest person he ever knew.

Holmes often remembers how she solved the problem. He has kept her photograph for many years. If he sees it, he will think of her again. She was different from other people he met.

The king returned to his country without trouble. He has told his family about Holmes’s help. If he needs help again, he will come back. But he hopes he will not need it.

Holmes still works on many cases. He has solved many hard problems since then. But he will always remember Irene Adler. She was the one who surprised him the most.

Irene Adler was not like most people. She was quick to understand things. She could see what others could not. Holmes knew she was special from the start.

### The Third Ingredient (B1)

In a big city, there was a small house on a quiet street. A young woman named Hetty lived there.

Hetty worked hard every day. She did not have much money left after she paid for her room. She often felt she needed more food because she could not buy enough.

One evening, Hetty came home tired. She had only a little money in her pocket. There was nothing in her small food place. She thought about the food her mother used to make. But she had no money to buy what she needed.

Hetty sat at her table. She did not know what to do. Then she heard a knock at the door.

It was her neighbor, Cecilia. Cecilia was a kind woman who lived alone. She held a small dish and smiled. She said, 'Hetty, I made too much food today.' 'I thought you might want some.'

Hetty thanked her and took the dish inside. The food smelled good. But she knew it was not enough for two meals. She decided to add water to make it last longer. As she mixed it, she heard another knock.

This time, it was Joe. Joe lived in the room above Hetty. He looked a bit quiet and held out a small bag. He said, 'I found this in my coat.' 'I do not know how it got there.'

Hetty took the bag and thanked him. She wondered why he gave it to her. She added what was inside to the food and let it cook.

Hetty thought it was strange. Both her neighbors had come to her door that evening. Just as she was about to eat, there was another knock.

It was Mrs. Flannigan, an old woman from down the hall. She held a small piece of meat. She said, 'I bought too much today.' 'I will not eat it all.'

Hetty took the meat and added it to the dish. Soon, the kitchen smelled very good. Hetty saw she had all the parts of a real meal. She laughed and thought she was lucky.

Hetty asked her neighbors to share the food. They all sat together and talked while they ate. The food was very good. Everyone said it was the best they had eaten in a long time.

Hetty felt happy and full. She was happy not just because of the food. She was happy because her friends were kind.

After that night, Hetty and her neighbors often ate together. They saw that if they helped each other, they would have enough. Hetty always remembered that evening. A simple thing from each person made a big meal.

The meal brought people together. Sometimes, the best things come from places you do not expect. If you share a little, you may get more than you give. Hetty knew this was true.

One day, Cecilia said, 'If we all bring something small, we can eat well.' Joe agreed and said, 'I will bring what I can.' Mrs. Flannigan smiled and said, 'I will too.' Hetty was glad they had started this new way.

Now, they meet often in the evening. They talk and laugh while they eat. Hetty feels she has more than before. She has good food and good friends.

The neighbors have learned something important. When people work together, life is better. Hetty is happy she knocked on their doors that first night. She knows they will continue to share.

Hetty still thinks about that evening sometimes. She has never forgotten how it felt to be so hungry. But now, she knows she is not alone. Her neighbors will always help if she needs it.

One morning, Hetty woke up early. She decided to make breakfast for everyone. She knocked on each door and invited them to come. Soon, the kitchen was full of laughter and good smells.

They all sat down to eat together. Hetty smiled as she watched her friends. She realized that sharing made everything better. It was not just about the food, but about the company too.

From that day on, they made a plan. Each person would bring something different each week. This way, they would always have enough to eat. They would never feel hungry again.

Hetty felt proud of what they had started. She knew it was a small thing, but it made a big difference. Her life had changed because of her neighbors. She was grateful for their kindness every day.
