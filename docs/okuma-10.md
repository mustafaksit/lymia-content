### The Gingerbread Man (B1)

Once upon a time, an old woman made a special meal shaped like a little man. She put the food into a hot machine. When she opened the door, the little man jumped out fast. He began to run away from the kitchen. The old woman was very surprised. She called for him to stop immediately. She wanted him to come back to her house. She looked at the open door with great shock. The fast runner laughed in a high voice. He told them he would never return. He ran past an old man who was working in the garden. He stated that he was too fast for anyone. The man tried to catch the little fellow. However, the runner was simply too quick. Further down the road, the proud meal met a farm animal. This farm animal also wanted to eat him.

The meal stated that he had run away from many different people. He said he would run away from the animal too. With every step he took, he felt more certain about his speed. He ran across fields and near a forest while singing a happy song. No one could ever catch him because his legs moved fast. He felt very happy about his new trip. He did not want to stop running at all. He thought he was the fastest person in the whole world. He passed many trees and small houses along the way. Children looked at him from their yards with wide eyes. They tried to catch him, but they failed completely. He just laughed and kept moving forward. His speed was truly amazing. Every single person missed him as he passed by. He felt like a king. If he ran fast, nobody would ever catch him. He had already crossed many fields today. He had never stopped for anyone. He enjoyed his fast journey very much.

Soon, the proud runner came near a deep river. A quiet animal was sitting by the water. It offered to help the little meal cross safely. Because the meal could not swim, he decided to trust the animal. He climbed onto its body. As they moved into the deep water, the animal spoke. The animal told the meal to move up onto its back. When they reached the middle, the animal gave another order. The animal told the meal to sit upon its head. Finally, the animal asked him to jump onto its face. The animal said this would keep him from getting cold. The moment the meal reached the face, the animal opened its mouth. The animal caught him. That was the end of the little man. He thought he was too bright for everyone. If he had known the truth, he would have stayed away. The clever fox had eaten him at last.

### A Retrieved Reformation (B1)

Jimmy was in prison for ten months. He opened strong boxes with special tools.

The prison boss told Jimmy he could leave early. He had to be good to get out.

Jimmy smiled and said he never opened boxes. That same day, the boss let Jimmy go.

Outside, Jimmy met his old friend Mike Dolan. Mike gave Jimmy his tools back.

Jimmy was happy to have his tools again. He went to work right away.

In less than three weeks, he opened boxes in three towns. The police started to look for him.

One day, Jimmy went to a small town called Elmore. He walked into the bank to ask about a room.

At the door, he saw a beautiful young woman. She was the bank owner’s child, Annabel.

Jimmy fell in love with her when he saw her smile. He decided to stay in Elmore.

Jimmy changed his name to Ralph Spencer. He opened a shoe shop in town.

The shop did very well, and he made many friends. After a year, Ralph was going to marry Annabel.

Everyone in Elmore liked Ralph. He felt happy and safe there.

One day, a man named Ben Price came to town. He knew Ralph was really Jimmy.

Ben watched Ralph but did not take him to prison. Ralph hoped Ben would leave him alone.

The next morning, Ralph and Annabel’s family went to the bank. Annabel’s little cousin May played near the new strong box.

Suddenly, May got shut inside the strong box. No one could open the special lock.

Ralph knew he was the only one who could save her. He looked at Annabel and then at the box.

If he opened it, Ben would take him to prison. But he could not let the child die.

Ralph took out his old tools and worked fast. In less than ten minutes, the box was open.

May was free, but Ralph saw Ben at the door. He knew his new life was over.

Ben looked at him and said, 'I do not know you, Mr. Spencer.' Then he turned and walked away.

Ralph stayed free because he saved the child. He had truly changed for love.

Jimmy had always been good with tools. He never thought he would use them to save a life.

Before he met Annabel, he only thought about himself. Now, he wanted to be a better man.

The people in Elmore did not know his past. They saw him as a kind and hard-working man.

Ralph worked hard every day in his shoe shop. He wanted to make Annabel proud.

He often thought about how his life had changed. He was happy and did not want to lose what he had.

When Ben Price came to town, Ralph felt worried. He knew Ben would not forget about him.

Ralph hoped he could keep his new life. He did not want to go back to prison.

The day May got shut in the box was very scary. Everyone in the bank tried to help her.

Annabel was very upset and cried for help. Ralph knew he had to do something.

He remembered the tools in his bag. He had not used them in a long time.

Ralph worked quickly and carefully. He did not want to make a mistake.

When the box opened, everyone was happy. May was safe and did not seem hurt.

Ralph looked at Ben and waited for him to act. He did not know what Ben would do.

Ben had seen everything. He knew Ralph was the one who opened the box.

But Ben did not take Ralph to prison. He walked away and left Ralph free.

Ralph was surprised but very happy. He could stay with Annabel and his new life.

From that day, Ralph worked even harder. He wanted to be the best man he could be.

Annabel loved him more than ever. She was proud of the man he had become.

The people in Elmore talked about what happened. They thought Ralph was very brave.

Ralph knew he had made the right choice. He would never go back to his old ways.

He had a new life and a new love. He would do anything to protect them.

### Mammon and the Archer (B1)

Richard Rockwall lived in New York. He was a young man.

His father, Anthony Rockwall, had a large business. He had a lot of money.

Richard loved a girl named Miss Lantry. She came from a good family.

But her family had little money now. Richard wanted to marry her.

He was afraid she might say no. He thought it was because his family was rich.

He also worried about another man named Kelly. Kelly was from her town.

Richard did not like Kelly. He thought Kelly might ask her first.

One day, Richard told his father about his problem. He said, 'If I do not ask her soon, Kelly will ask her first.'

His father laughed and said, 'Money can solve any problem.' Richard did not believe money could help with love.

Anthony had a brother before. This brother was very rich too.

But he did not like Anthony. The brother had a son named Tom.

Tom was not a good man. Richard did not like him either.

Richard’s brother died the year before. He left all his money to Tom.

But Tom did not get the money yet. Anthony wanted to use this to help Richard.

He thought of a plan. He wanted to make sure Richard could ask Miss Lantry first.

Anthony called a man named Archer. Archer worked for him.

Archer was very good at solving problems with money. Anthony said, 'I need you to stop the traffic in New York for one hour.'

Archer was surprised but said he would try. The next day, Richard was to meet Miss Lantry at a party.

Anthony wanted to make sure Richard got there on time. He told Archer to stop all the cars, buses, and trains.

Archer did not know why but he started his work. At the same time, Richard was in a taxi.

He was on his way to the party. The streets were very busy.

The taxi could not move fast. Richard was worried he would be late.

He thought, 'If I am late, Kelly will ask her first.' Suddenly, all the traffic stopped.

Cars, buses, and people could not move at all. Richard’s taxi was stuck too.

But he did not wait. He got out and started to run.

He ran as fast as he could through the empty streets. He reached the party just in time.

Miss Lantry was there. No one else had asked her yet.

He went to her and said, 'I love you.' 'Will you marry me?' he asked.

Miss Lantry smiled and said yes. Richard was very happy.

He thanked his father later. Anthony laughed and said, 'Money can do anything, even help love.'

Richard knew his father was right. He was glad it worked.

The next day, people talked about the strange traffic stop. No one knew why it happened.

But it helped Richard. Archer never told anyone about his part in the plan.

Richard and Miss Lantry lived happily after that. They got married soon.

Richard started to work in his father’s business. He learned a lot about money and plans.

Miss Lantry helped him see love was important too. They often talked about their future together.

Anthony was happy his plan worked. He was glad his son found love.

Kelly never got a chance to ask Miss Lantry. He left town soon after.

Tom finally got his money but it did not make him happy. He spent it all on bad things.

Richard and Miss Lantry had a big party for their wedding. Many people came to celebrate.

They lived in a nice house in New York. They had two children after a few years.

Richard’s father visited them often. He loved his grandchildren very much.

Miss Lantry’s family was happy too. They had little money but a lot of love.

Richard always remembered how he ran through the empty streets. It was the day he found his true love.

He often told the story to his children. They loved to hear about their parents’ love.

The traffic stop became a funny family story. Everyone laughed when they heard it.

Richard was glad his father helped him. He knew love was the most important thing.
