# Okuma Raporu 1

Bu partide 8 hikaye tamamlandi.

## Basliklar + ozetler

- **The Crow and the Pitcher** (classic): A thirsty person finds a deep water pot and tries to reach the water.
- **The Ransom of Red Chief** (adventure): Two poor men kidnap a young boy, but he treats the crime like a game.
- **The Bremen Town Musicians** (adventure): Four animal friends travel together to make music and find a new home.
- **The Elephant's Child** (adventure): A curious young animal leaves home to find answers and starts a strange river adventure.
- **Rikki-Tikki-Tavi** (adventure): A brave small mongoose helps a kind family and fights two bad snakes.
- **The Legend of Sleepy Hollow** (horror): A nervous school man rides home at night and a strange headless rider follows him.
- **The Cop and the Anthem** (daily): A homeless man tries to get arrested for the winter, but his plans keep failing.
- **The Celebrated Jumping Frog** (daily): Jim Smiley trains a special jumping frog, but a stranger makes a secret plan.

### The Crow and the Pitcher (B1)

One hot summer day, a very active bird flew across dry land to find water. After a long search, he discovered a tall container. The container had a little water at the bottom. The bird was extremely happy because he hoped to drink and feel better. He wanted to feel good after such a long flight under the hot sun. The day was very difficult for any small animal. He looked around the dry land to see what else he might find. There was no river or lake near the open field. He only had this one chance to get some water. The bird flew down toward the container with great care. He felt sure that he could find a way to reach the water inside. His bright eyes looked at the bottom of the tall container. He saw the water waiting for him. It looked cool and clean. He knew that he must try to drink it. The sun continued to send hot light upon the dry ground. He needed to act fast before the water disappeared. His little heart beat fast as he landed near the container. He always worked hard when life became difficult.

However, a big problem appeared when he tried to reach the water. The top of the container was too narrow. The water level was much too low for his mouth. He tried to push his head inside. He could not touch the cool drink. Many birds would have given up and left the place in grief. This smart bird, who had a very strong mind, refused to stop. Instead of walking away, he stood quiet. He began to think about how he could solve the difficulty. He knew that a smart mind can find a way through any problem. He looked at the narrow top again and again. He tried to think of something useful to do. The water was right there, but he could not reach it with his mouth. He did not want to leave without a drink. His family always told him to be strong when things got hard. He remembered those good words now. He stayed right where he was to look for a solution. The hot wind blew across the dry ground. He had experienced many hard days before this moment. If he fails today, he will not survive the afternoon.

Soon, his bright eyes saw some small rocks resting on the ground near the green land. An idea came to him which would change the situation completely. He picked up a rock one by one. He held each piece in his mouth. He carried it carefully toward the container. With great care, the bird dropped the small rocks into the open top of the glass. As each piece fell down into the bottom, the water started to rise higher and higher. The smart animal continued this work until the water reached the top. Finally, the bird was able to drink easily. He could satisfy his terrible need for water. This simple story shows everyone that care and smart thinking can solve a very hard problem. When people use their minds, even the most difficult situation can be managed. The bird felt very proud of his work. He had solved the big problem all by himself. The cool water made him feel strong again. He flew away into the blue sky with a happy heart. His bright day ended with a great success. If he has time, he will tell his friends about this journey. He has learned that a smart mind brings great rewards.

### The Ransom of Red Chief (B1)

Two men needed money for their life. They had a big problem with debt. They decided to take a young boy. The child was the son of a rich man in town. They thought the parents would pay a large amount of money. They planned everything carefully during the week. If they have enough time, they will get the money easily. They have made a plan to catch the child. The town is small, and the people are friendly. However, the men have large problems with their business. They must find cash to pay back people. If they take the boy, they will solve everything. The father has a lot of money in the bank. The men hope for a good result from this action.

Late one night, the friends took the child from his home. The boy called himself Red Chief and felt very happy. He thought camping in the woods was a fun game. He ran around all night while the men tried to sleep. The men wanted peace, but the boy had other plans. They have lived through a difficult night already. If the boy continues to run, the men will lose their minds completely. The woods are dark and cold during the night hours. The boy does not feel any fear at all. He sings songs and makes a lot of noise. The men try to rest on the ground. If he makes more noise, they will stand up again. They have spent hours trying to catch him. The child is too fast for them.

In the morning, Red Chief began to bother them. He acted like a great Indian chief in a story. He ran after the men and hit one man with wood. The poor men were very tired and had no energy left. They felt completely worn out by his actions. He has caused many problems since the sun came up. If he hits them again, they will leave the woods immediately. The morning sun brings light to the green trees. The boy holds a stick in his hand. He pretends to fight an enemy in the camp. The men look at him with tired eyes. If he stops the game, they will feel happy. They have had enough trouble with this little kid. The situation is quite bad for both friends.

The boy did not want to go home at all. He wanted dangerous games every single hour. Both men became completely tired from the constant noise. They wrote a letter to the father of the boy. They asked for less money than before. They have tried to send him back several times today. If the father refuses, they will run away by themselves. The letter takes a long time to write down. They use a pen and a piece of paper. The message explains the demand for cash. If the father wants his son back, he must pay. The men hope the family will accept the terms. They have waited too long for a reply. The whole day passes with more trouble.

The good father refused to pay any money to them. Instead, he offered a different plan to the men. He wanted the men to pay him two hundred and fifty dollars. The tired men gladly gave the money to send the boy away. They were happy to leave the child with his family. They have learned a valuable lesson about taking children. If they ever need money again, they will find another way. The father knows his son is a lot of trouble. He wants the men to pay him for taking care of the boy. The men agree to the strange plan quickly. If they give the cash, they can finally escape. They have lost money, but they gain peace. The family takes the child back inside the house. The two friends walk away from the town forever.

### The Bremen Town Musicians (B1)

Once upon a time, an old horse worked for a master. The master did not want the horse now. Because the horse was old, he decided to leave. He wanted to become an artist in a large town. Along the road, he met a tired dog. The dog had been cast aside by its owner. The dog joined the horse on the road. Together they continued their journey toward the town. After some more time, they met an old cat. The life of the cat had grown very difficult. The cat decided to travel with the group. He wanted a fresh start as an artist too. Soon after, the three friends found a bird. The bird was crying by a farm. The bird explained that his master was mean. Then the bird joined their company. The four animals walked through the land. Night arrived and the forest became dark. They needed a place to sleep. So they looked around for a building. Soon, they saw a warm house. There was bright light in the middle of the trees. The horse walked up to the window. They looked inside to see what was happening.

Inside the house, a group of bad men sat around a table. The table was full of wonderful food and drinks. The animals wanted that food. They made a plan to push the bad men away. The horse stood at the bottom. The dog climbed onto his back. The cat sat on the dog. And the bird flew up to the top. When they made a terrible sound together, the glass broke. The forest was full of their shouts. The bad men were afraid of the strange sound. They ran away into the dark woods. The four happy artists entered the warm house. They ate the food left behind. They enjoyed the house so much. They never reached the town. Instead, they lived happily together in their new home. They stayed there for the rest of their days.
