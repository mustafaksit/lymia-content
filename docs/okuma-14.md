- **The Remarkable Rocket** (classic): A proud and boastful rocket believes he is the most important star of the show.
- **The Star-Child (tek epizod)** (classic): People find a poor child in the forest who grows up to be very mean.

- **The Diamond Maker** (mystery): A poor old man offers a tiny mysterious object he says he creates.

- **The Lion and the Gnat** (classic): A tiny bug dares to confront a mighty lion, thinking it can win.
- **The Tortoise and the Eagle** (classic): A little creature longs to fly and begs a powerful bird for a chance.

## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### The Star-Child (tek epizod) (B1)

Once upon a time, men found a little baby boy inside a forest. This happened during a cold night. The child was dressed in gold clothes and had a face like a star. They brought him home, and the man raised him as his own son. Many years passed by while the boy grew older and bigger every single day. He had everything he could ever want in life, and people loved his looks. They always told him that he was the most beautiful child in the entire world. The boy believed these words and thought he was better than anyone else around him.

As the star-child grew up, he became very proud and hard to other people. He thought that he was better than everyone else because of his beauty. He laughed at poor children and animals, treating them with total bad behavior. He never showed any respect to anyone who did not look as fine as him. His heart became cold, and he did not care about anybody else in the town.

One day, a poor woman came to the village looking for her lost child. She claimed that the star-child was her son, but he refused to accept her. He treated her with terrible force, saying she was too bad to be his mother. He shouted at her and told her to leave the village right away. The poor woman cried and walked away into the dark forest with a broken heart.

Immediately, his beauty changed, and his face became like that of a fish and a bird. When he saw himself in a pool of water, he realized something. He realized how bad his behavior had been. He saw his new face and felt terrible pain deep inside his mind and soul. He decided to walk across the world until he found his mother and earned her love. He knew that he must change his ways if he wanted to fix his life.

During his long journey, he suffered greatly and learned how to be kind to everyone. He helped animals in trouble and shared his food with the poor people. He walked through many towns and forests while looking everywhere for his mother. Many months passed, and he always tried to do good things for other people. He finally understood why his face had changed and why his heart was wrong.

Finally, his mother appeared, and his true beauty returned because his heart was finally good. She held him and told him that she gave him back his place. She spoke sweet words to him and loved him completely. The young man smiled and thanked her with tears running down his face. He lived a long and happy life, always helping people and being kind to everyone. Everyone in the village respected him because he had become a truly good person.

### The Diamond Maker (B1)

Late one evening, a strange thing happened on a dark street. A strange man approached me while I walked home. I walked through the quiet neighborhood during the night. He looked very tired and poor, but his eyes were bright. Without warning, the man spoke to me in a low voice. He told me that he had made something of enormous value. I have always remembered that night because it was very unusual. Many people live through similar moments and never know the truth.

At first, I thought the person was not thinking right. I thought he was just playing a funny game. However, the stranger pulled a small glass object from his pocket. He showed it to me under the light of the street fire. He claimed that he had made the stone himself inside a room. Great heat and pressure were used to create this tiny item. I listened carefully while the man described the long process of his work. If the stranger returns, I will listen to him again. I have never forgotten his words about that amazing process.

He explained that many months had been spent studying science books. He studied in a cold house alone. Simple machines were built by his own hands to test the secret method. Though his family suffered from lack of money, he never stopped trying. He always tried to succeed in his work. Suddenly, the stranger looked around the dark street with deep fear in his eyes. He told me that other people wanted to steal his secret method. Because of this danger, he had experienced many problems.

Because of this danger, he had to move constantly from one place to another. He asked if I would buy the stone for a very low price. He needed money badly for food. I looked at the bright object again, wondering if his wild story could be true. The stone maker seemed very worried about cash to pay for food. Yet, the risk was high, and I had no way to prove the value. Therefore, I decided to refuse the offer. If I had bought the object, I would have taken a big risk.

I walked away down the street and left him behind. To this day, that strange meeting remains a complete problem in my mind. I often wonder if that poor man actually created stones or if it was a lie. If I buy something like that, I will check the facts first. I have always wondered about that night. Many people live through similar moments and never know the truth. If the stranger returns, I will listen to him again. Perhaps he will show me another wonderful object.

### The Nightingale and the Ant (mesel) (B1)

Once upon a time, a little worker lived through the warm summer season. Every single day, she labored hard to prepare for the coming winter. While other animals rested, she collected food to save for later days. She walked across the green ground, carrying heavy food back to her home. She knew that if she did not save food, she would suffer later. She wanted to be ready so that she could survive the cold months. She worked from morning until evening without taking any long breaks. Her house became full of food because she planned for the future well. Every piece of food brought her closer to a safe and secure winter time. The little worker always remembered that preparation is important for survival. She never stopped moving forward with her daily tasks and duties. Life required constant effort, and she understood that very clearly.

High up in a tree, a singer sat and made wonderful music all day long. The bird loved to make music and believed that singing was the best thing. When the singer saw the worker laboring, he laughed at her. He asked why she spent her time working instead of enjoying the weather. The worker looked up at the singer and explained her plan carefully. She told him that winter would come soon and food would be hard to find. She warned him that if he did not gather food now, he would have nothing. He would have nothing to eat when the snow arrived. However, the singer just shook his head and continued singing his sweet songs. He thought that the warm weather would last forever and ever. Music was his only interest, and he cared about nothing else. He spent his hours in the tree while the days passed quickly by.

Months passed quickly, and the warm summer days finally came to an end. The weather turned very cold, and heavy snow covered the ground everywhere. The singer could no longer find any food to eat in the snow. He felt extremely weak because he had nothing in his stomach. He realized that his beautiful songs could not save him from the cold. Remembering the little worker, the poor bird went to her house. He arrived at her door and asked for help with a quiet voice. He admitted that he had made a big mistake by ignoring the future. He knew that he had failed to prepare for the difficult season. The kind worker opened her door and gave the poor singer some food to eat. She showed mercy because she wanted him to live through the winter. From that day on, the singer learned a very important lesson about life.

The singer finally understood that there is a time for work and a time for music. He realized that balance is necessary for a good and safe existence. Never again would he waste his summer days on only one activity. He decided that he would help the worker when the next year began. The winter months slowly passed, and spring returned to the green land. The snow melted away, and the warm sun shone upon the happy forest. The singer and the worker lived peacefully side by side from then on. They understood each other better because of the difficult winter experience. Every season brought new duties, but they always managed them well together. The story of their friendship became known to all the animals around them. Everyone learned that hard work and planning are valuable tools for survival. Thus, the forest remained a peaceful and happy place for everyone there.
