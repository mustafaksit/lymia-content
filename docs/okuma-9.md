## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### Stone Soup (B1)

Two tired people walked into a small town after a long journey. They had no food left in bags. Bodies felt very empty. People knocked on several doors. Local individuals refused to share anything. Every person said that they had little food. If nobody would offer a meal, the group would remain empty of energy. People have tried to find another way. Since nobody would offer a meal, bright people made a plan. They gathered dry wood and built a big fire in the center of the street. Then they placed a large container of water over it. After water became very hot, one person took a clean stone from the ground. He dropped it carefully into the container. People have shown that water and stone can start a meal. If people look closely, they will see the start of wonderful food. They have made a fire. They have found water. They have put a stone in water. Many hours passed by the fire. The water was very hot now. The stone stayed down in the water. People watched the street. They wanted to see what would happen next. A strange neighbor walked over and looked into the container. She asked what was happening. The man smiled and explained that they were making wonderful stone food. He added that food would taste even better if someone had a little extra item. Interested by the idea, the neighbor went home quickly. She brought back some extra things and a few plant parts. Soon, another neighbor came close. People told her that plants would make the stone dish rich. The woman agreed, returning shortly with sweet roots and round roots. As a nice smell spread through the town, more people came out with meat and other food items. Everyone contributed something to the container. The food was shared among the whole group. Neighbors finally understood that working together could create a great party from a single stone.

### East of the Sun and West of the Moon (B1)

Once upon a time, a poor mother lived with her lovely child. They had a small house near the woods. One cold winter day, a great white bear came to their door. The animal offered a rich life if the young girl would go with him. Although she felt afraid, she agreed because her family needed money. She climbed upon his back for a long journey. Every day they traveled through the deep woods together. The animal walked with her for many hours without a stop. She held onto him hard because the cold wind blew all around them. They finally reached a quiet place where they could rest for the night. The bear was very kind to her during their entire time in the forest. She slowly began to feel safe with the large animal. They shared food and water before they went to sleep. The child knew that her family would receive the money they needed. This thought gave her calm while she closed her eyes. The white snow covered the ground outside their resting place. Morning came quickly, and they started their travel again.

Every night after everyone else went to sleep, the bear turned into a man. He spoke softly to her in the dark room. However, he told her that she must never look at his face with a light. For a time they lived well together, but soon her mother gave her a small light. Late one night, the child lit the light to see him. Then drops of hot water fell upon his skin. The man woke up with great pain. He explained that an enemy had placed a spell upon him. Because she broke her promise, he had to leave immediately. He went away to a high building far east of the sun. He went west of the sky too. The young girl woke up alone in a strange forest. She had no idea how to find him again. During her long and difficult travel, she met old women. They gave her wonderful items to help her on the way. She used these special things to trade with friendly people. Those people helped her cross the sea and the high mountains. She walked for many days and never stopped moving forward. The sun was hot during the day, and the night brought freezing cold air. She felt tired, but her strong will kept her going. Every person she met along the way gave her hope. She walked across the country and never stopped.

Finally, she arrived at the high building where the enemy kept the man. The enemy planned to marry him against his will. The girl used her fruit to trade with the woman. She washed the water spots off the shirt as only a true love could do. The spell broke completely. The man and his wife escaped together. They lived well ever after in their new home. Their friends came to visit them and shared great joy. The long journey was finally at an end for the two friends. They looked back at the difficult times with a smile. No more trouble could ever hurt them again. The sun gave light brightly upon their good faces every single day. They built a wonderful life together and shared everything. The story of their love spread across the entire country. People talked about the strong girl who crossed the mountains. Her love had won every single problem in the end. She found peace and joy in her new life.

### The Three Billy Goats Gruff (B1)

Once upon a time, three brother animals needed food. Their field had very little green plant stuff. Across the river, a wonderful high hill waited for them. If they crossed, they could eat and grow strong. However, a big and bad person lived under the bridge. They have lived in that small place for a long time. Every single day, they looked across the water with great hope. They wanted to find a better home on the other side. The smallest brother was the first to cross the bridge. His tiny feet went step by step over the wood boards. The bad person shouted from below in a big voice. Who is walking across my bridge, cried the angry animal. I will eat you up for my dinner right now.

The little animal was not happy, but he spoke in a good voice. Please do not eat me, he said with care. My second brother, who is much bigger, will cross right after me. The person thought about this and let him go. He knew that a bigger meal was coming soon. Soon, the second brother walked onto the bridge. His steps went back and forth with a heavy sound. The bad person ran out again from his dark home. Who is that on my bridge, said the strange character. I am going to catch you now and eat you.

The medium animal replied quickly, do not take me yet. My big brother is coming behind me. He is much larger and stronger than both of us. The person agreed to wait because he wanted a much bigger meal. He let the second brother cross safely to the other side. At last, the biggest brother arrived at the bridge. His heavy steps shook the ground with a strong sound. The bad person jumped up onto the road to stop him. Who is walking across my bridge now, he said angrily. The biggest brother did not feel bad at all.

With a big push from his strong head, he hit the person hard. The bad person fell down into the deep water below. He was never seen again by anyone in the world. The three brothers lived happily on the high hill. From that day forward, the animals could cross the river without any trouble. They ate the fresh plant stuff and enjoyed their new home. No other animal ever tried to stop them on the bridge again. They have found peace and safety in their new life. If they stay here, they will always have enough food to eat.
