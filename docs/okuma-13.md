### The Book of Dragons: The Book of Beasts (B1)

Once upon a time, a young boy found a very old book in his house. He sat in a quiet room with the strange book. The book had dark paper and special words on every single page. He opened the heavy cover with his hand. Amazing things began to happen right before his eyes. Suddenly, amazing things flew out from the pages. They moved around the house very fast. There were small birds and other strange animals. The animals came alive into the real world. The good child knew a big problem was here. He must control these animals before they caused trouble. Some animals tried to run away from him. Other animals jumped onto the table. The good boy did not feel afraid at all. The situation was difficult to manage alone. He tried to catch each animal carefully. He wanted everything to become safe again.

He was quick and strong. The young person managed to guide the animals. He pushed them back toward the book. Every single animal was placed inside the pages. They belonged there before the evening arrived. The house finally became quiet again. The special book closed itself fast on the table. The boy sat down with a happy smile. He knew that he had done a wonderful job. He decided to keep the book in a safe place. He wanted no other trouble to happen. That classic day ended well for him. He went to sleep with sweet dreams. He dreamed of special stories. If he ever opens the book again, he will see something new. He will be ready for any new surprise that appears.

Many hours passed while he thought about the magic book. He has learned an important lesson about strange things. If he finds another mystery, he will stay very calm. His mother walked into the room to check on him. She saw the book resting quietly on the wooden table. The boy told her nothing about the wonderful creatures. He kept the secret deep inside his heart. The night grew dark outside the small window. He closed his eyes and slept until the morning light. Life went back to normal for the young child. He has kept the room clean and tidy since then. No other problems have appeared in the house. He always remembers the day the animals came alive. He knows that books hold many powerful secrets inside. If someone asks him about it, he will just smile. The old book remains closed upon the wooden table.

### Heidi (tek epizod) (B1)

High up in the mountains, Heidi lived with her old man in a small home. Every morning, the sun gave clear light over the green grass and the tall trees. Heidi loved to wake up early and run outside to look at the wonderful world. Her old man watched her with a nice smile from the door. Soon, they would walk together up the high mountain path to find the animals. The friendly animals ate the fresh grass while the little girl played in the warm air. During the afternoon, the old man prepared a simple meal of food and milk. They sat together on a big stone and ate. The wind blew through the trees. Heidi loved her old man and the high mountains more than anything else in the world. When the sun began to go down, they walked back down to their simple home. They have lived there for years. If the weather is good, they will walk up the hill again tomorrow.

Every single day brought new happiness to the little girl. She always woke up before the sun came into the sky. The air was clean and cold in the early morning hours. Heidi put on her clothes and ran out the door immediately. She wanted to see her friends on the mountain. The animals were always waiting for her near the high path. They made happy sounds when they saw her coming. Heidi brought food for them every single morning. She had learned how to care for them from the old man. They spent hours playing in the open space. Sometimes they ran across the green grass together. The old man watched them from the door with a calm heart. He knew that the child was happy in this place. After the morning play, they always walked further up the rocks. The view from the top was amazing. They could see the whole world below them. Every plant and tree looked small from that top part. Heidi pointed at the far towns and asked many questions. The old man answered every single one with great care. He taught her the names of the birds and the trees. They shared everything they knew about the mountains. When the sun climbed high into the sky, it became very warm. That was the time to find a place in the forest. They sat down near a large rock to rest their feet. The old man took out the food and milk from his bag. They ate their simple meal in silence. The air smelled of fresh plants and earth. Heidi felt safe and warm near her family. She had never known any other life than this one. The mountains were her true home. After they finished eating, they sat for a long time. They watched the water move slowly across the sky. The world was quiet and calm around them. Heidi often stopped with her head on the stone. The old man would cover her with a warm coat. He stayed close to protect her from the wind. When she woke up, the afternoon was already ending. The light turned gold upon the high rocks. It was time to make their way back down the path. They gathered the animals and started the journey home. The dark lines grew long behind them as they walked. They reached the small house just as the stars appeared. Inside, the rooms were dark and cool. The old man prepared a small fire to keep them warm. They sat near the light until it was time for sleep. Heidi closed her eyes and dreamed of the high mountains. She knew tomorrow would bring another wonderful day. The mountains always welcomed them back with open arms. Life was simple, but it was full of love and peace.

### A Little Princess (tek epizod) (B1)

Sara was a very sweet child who lived in a large house. She lived there during a cold winter. She always showed great care to everyone around her. She did this even when times were hard. People in the school liked her. Her smile brought fun into dark rooms. Her heart was full of love. She wanted to help people every day. Many friends talked to her because she listened well. She gave good advice to them. Her positive energy made the school a much better place. It was better for all young students. Even when teachers asked hard questions, Sara tried to give the right answer. She walked through halls with a bright face. She had a warm heart for all. No one ever saw her look bad or sad. This happened during those long winter months. She believed that being nice was the most important thing to do in life.

One day, a big problem changed her life. Her family had no money left after this happened. Although she felt deep worry, Sara kept her calm spirit. She tried to help others every day. She would share her little food with poor people on the street. This proved her good heart during difficult months. She faced many challenges during this time. However, her good actions were watched by friends. These friends wanted to support her. Even though things were hard, she never stopped being nice. She was nice to people in need. Her sweet nature burned brightly. People began to notice her inner strength. A nice man from across the street saw her care. He brought wonderful gifts for her. This support made her life better. It showed that good actions are always rewarded. Neighbors brought small meals to ensure the family had enough food to eat. Sara thanked every single person who came to their door. She always smiled at them. Her parents felt proud of their young daughter. They thanked God for her. They knew the family would survive this hard period. They stayed together to face the problem. Friends from school visited to bring old books and clothes. These gifts helped them a lot. Every gift made a big difference in their daily life. It also gave them hope. At the end of the story, Sara remained happy. She shared love with everyone. Her amazing journey proved that being kind matters most. It matters most during bad times. People in town talked about her sweet heart for many years. This happened after winter. She grew up to be a strong woman. She always put other people first. If someone needed help, Sara was the first person at their door. Her story became a lesson for all children. These children went to school there. They learned that a simple smile could change a dark day into light. Sara never forgot where she came from. She never forgot the people who helped her family. She gave back to the community by working at a local center. Her life was a clear example of how love and care beat problems. Today, people still remember the sweet child. She brought light to town. Her memory lives on in the hearts of everyone. They knew her then. We can all learn from her actions. We should try to be better friends. The world is a better place because people like Sara live here. May we always follow her path and share our love with everyone.
