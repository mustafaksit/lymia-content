### Twenty Thousand Leagues (tek epizod) (B1)

Many people across the world talked about a strange animal moving quickly through water. Some workers believed it was a big fish, while others thought a machine had been built. A boat went out to find the truth. A professor joined the journey with his friend. They traveled for days until something hit their boat. Both men fell into the cold water. Many hours passed before they woke up on a strange ship. They had never seen anything like this wonderful metal machine before today. The car moved smoothly under the waves without making any sound. It seemed very unusual for such a huge structure to exist in the deep water. They wondered how people could build such a fine machine. Every part of the ship worked with great power. The professor felt both excited and worried about what would happen next.

When the professor opened his eyes, he found himself inside a huge metal room called Nautilus. A man named Nemo appeared and explained he lived under water away from society. Although they were inside, the leader allowed them to walk around and see many rooms. The professor was shown wonderful equipment which made it possible to breathe and study local life. They learned how the machine cleaned air and created fresh water for everyone on board. The rooms were filled with books and scientific instruments from all over the world. Nemo showed them beautiful collections of sea items kept in glass cases. Each space was designed with great care and attention to detail. The professor spent hours looking at items he had only read about in books. His friend also enjoyed seeing the different things collected from deep water. They had already spent several days learning about how the ship operated. Everything inside felt like part of a strange and interesting dream.

During their time on the machine, they traveled across deep water and saw amazing things. They walked on the dark floor of the sea wearing special clothes. Bright light came from above to show them the path. Fishes swam past large glass windows as the boat moved forward without stopping. They observed strange plants and wonderful animals living in the dark water. The pressure was heavy, but their suits protected them from any danger. If anyone had asked them about the experience, they would have said it felt like something special. The beauty of the water world left both men completely quiet. They recorded notes about every new animal and plant they encountered. Days turned into weeks while they explored places no human had ever visited. The journey felt long as they moved from one deep region to another. They had never imagined that such a wonderful world existed under the waves.

However, Nemo kept many secrets and never spoke about his past life on land. Days passed, and the atmosphere inside the machine became very strange and dark. The leader seemed angry with the world, and he wanted no connection to other people. His behavior changed slowly, which made the professor and his friend feel worried about the future. They noticed that Nemo avoided talking to them for long periods of time. The man often stared through the large windows into the empty dark water. His feeling grew colder with each passing day inside the metal ship. The professor tried to speak with him, but the man walked away without answering. They realized something was very wrong with the mind of their host. Fear began to grow inside their hearts as they watched his strange actions. They knew they could not stay in this place anymore. Plans for an escape started to form in their minds.

One night, a bad storm came. The boat traveled near a dangerous part of the water. The professor watched through a window as big waves hit the water around them. While the captain was busy, the two men decided that they must escape from the machine. They gathered some food and essential items for their dangerous journey ahead. They found a small boat. They left the Nautilus behind. They finally reached safety on a nearby land. The waves were huge, but they fought hard to keep the small boat moving forward. After hours of struggle, their small boat finally touched the land of an island. They looked back at the dark water where the metal ship had disappeared. Both men felt deeply thankful that they had survived the bad event. They knew they would always remember their great journey under the sea. If anyone asked them about it, they would tell the true story.

### The Time Machine (tek epizod) (B1)

The traveler sat before his friends and told a story about a wonderful journey. He had spent many years building a special machine which could move through time. The device was made of bright metal and small glass pieces. According to him, this machine would take any person into the future. His friends listened closely while he explained how the vehicle worked. He pulled a small metal handle, and the room began to turn around him. Days and nights passed quickly like a fast movement of light. Finally, the machine stopped, and he stepped out into a strange new world. The future world was filled with beautiful green trees and strange little people. Those people lived a very easy life without any serious work or trouble. They did not care about books or science because everything was provided for them. However, the traveler soon noticed that something was deeply wrong. Another group of living things stayed under the dark ground. These lower beings were very dangerous during the night hours. They came up to take the surface people away into the dark. The traveler tried to protect his new friends from those terrible things.

After a difficult struggle, he managed to find his machine again. He quickly jumped inside the seat and pulled the main handle hard. The vehicle moved back through time until it reached his own home. Although his friends did not believe the story, he knew that the journey had been real. He has always remembered the strange world he visited. If he goes back again, he will bring more help. The machine has stayed in his room for many months. He has never told another person the complete truth about the machine. His friends have often asked him about the journey. He has just smiled and said nothing more to them. Perhaps they will understand the truth at that time. Until then, he keeps the machine safe in his house. He sits by the window and thinks about the wonderful green trees. The little people live in that strange world without any care. The lower beings stay under the ground and wait for the night. He must protect the weak people from those dangerous animals. His heart beats fast when he remembers the dark place. He will prepare more tools before he makes the next trip. The room is quiet while he plans the new journey. No one knows what will happen when he starts the engine again. He hopes that his friends will finally see the truth. Until that day arrives, he stays home and keeps the secret safe.

He has thought about the strange place every single day. His friends have often wondered about the truth of his words. If they listen to him, they will learn something important. The journey changed his life in a very deep way. He has planned to make another trip very soon. The machine is ready for the next test. He will check every single part before he starts. The future world waits for him in the dark. He knows that he must be strong and brave. His friends will stay behind while he travels alone. Maybe they will understand after he brings back proof. The room remains quiet as he looks outside the window. The sun goes down and the night begins to fall. He touches the metal handle of his time machine. He remembers the green trees and the little people. He also remembers the lower beings under the ground. He will prepare more food and tools for the trip. Everything must be ready before the engine starts. He hopes for a better result this time. The secret stays safe inside his heart and mind. He will wait until the right moment arrives. Then he will travel into the future once again.

### Aepyornis Island (B1)

Adada was a man who lived a quiet life on a strange island. One day, he found a big egg in the land. The egg was very heavy and came from an ancient animal. Adada took the egg home because he felt interested in it. He has always wanted to discover new things on the island. If he finds something special, he brings it to his house. This time, the egg was larger than any stone he had ever seen before. He has spent many years looking for such wonderful objects. He has always walked near the water to find new items. Every single morning, he looks around the ground for something new. The island is very quiet and peaceful all the time. Adada enjoys his life there without any trouble or doubt.

After several weeks, something amazing happened in the house. The cover broke open, and a tiny bird came out. It had long legs and a funny head, which made Adada smile. He decided to name the animal Aepyornis. He has cared for the bird since the first day it arrived. If the bird wants food, Adada always gives it fresh meals. They have shared many quiet mornings together near the water. The bird has grown up under the warm sun of the island. Adada has fed the bird every single day since it was born. The bird has stayed inside the small house for many weeks. It has always been happy to see Adada every morning. They have built a strong friendship during this peaceful time.

As days passed, the bird grew bigger and bigger. They became good partners and walked together near the water. Aepyornis liked to follow Adada everywhere he went on the island. Adada fed the animal every morning with fresh food from the land. He has watched the bird grow into a massive size over the months. If anyone visits the island, they will see how tall the bird has become. The animal has always stayed close to Adada during the day. They have traveled across many parts of the land together. The bird has never tried to run away from its good friend. Adada has always looked after the animal with great care. They have walked across the land in the warm sun. The massive bird is now a very important part of his life.

However, the bird became too large for the small house. It needed more space to run and play outside. Adada built a big room so that the animal would stay safe. Even though Aepyornis was strong, it remained very calm with Adada. He has worked hard to keep the animal safe from any danger. If the bird wants to run, it has plenty of room inside the new area. Adada has never felt worried about the large animal. He has built a strong wall around the space to protect it. The new area gives the bird enough room to play all day. Adada has spent many hours making the place safe. The animal has lived happily inside this large space. Adada has always known how to keep the bird secure.

The relationship between the man and the big bird grew deep. They spent many hours together under the sun and the stars. No other person lived on that quiet island with them. Their strange life continued in peace until the very end. He has enjoyed every moment of life with his unusual friend. If they stay together, they will live in peace for many more years. The story of Adada and Aepyornis has become a famous book on the island. He has shared this wonderful life with the bird for a long time. They have spent many years together in this quiet place. The memory of their friendship will stay alive forever. Adada has always loved his big bird partner very much. Their peaceful life on the strange island continues every day.
