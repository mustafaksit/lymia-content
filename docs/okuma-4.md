### Belling the Cat (B1)

Long ago, a group of mice lived in a large old house. They had one major problem. This problem caused them constant fear every single day. A big black cat lived in the same building. She was always hunting them. At any time the mice left their home to find food, the cat would catch them. Life became extremely difficult. The mice decided to hold a meeting. They needed a plan to protect themselves from this terrible danger. Many mice stood up to discuss the situation during the long evening. Some suggested hiding deeper inside the walls. That did not solve anything. Finally, a young mouse stepped forward with an interesting idea. He explained that if they put a small metal object around the cat, they would hear her. If the animal came near, the object would make a sound. Then every mouse could run away quickly before the danger arrived. All the other mice were very happy when they heard this wonderful proposal. They felt very glad because the plan seemed so simple and effective. However, an older mouse stood up and asked a very important question. He wanted to know which person would actually place the object on the cat. Silence fell across the room immediately as everyone thought about the problem. Not a single mouse wanted to take such a huge risk. They all knew that approaching the big cat meant certain death. In the end, talking about a good solution was much easier than doing it. Every mouse went back to hiding in the dark walls. The big cat continued to live in the house without any trouble. The mice learned that having a great idea is only the first step. Doing the actual work is much harder than speaking about it. They still must be careful every single day.

Many days passed since that meeting. The mice have tried to find other ways to stay safe. They have looked into every corner of the dark walls. They have talked about the cat again and again. Yet nobody has found a better answer. The big cat has remained in the same old building. She has continued to hunt them without any pity. The little mice have understood that fear is a heavy load. They have realized that words are easy to share. Actions are much harder to perform. Every single mouse has learned a hard lesson. They have accepted their difficult life in the house. They have stayed quiet when the sun goes down. They have watched the doors and windows with great care. The danger has never gone away. The black cat has stayed close to their home. The mice have kept themselves hidden in the dark. They have lived with constant worry in their hearts. They have known that only real action could save them. Since nobody has taken the risk, the problem has remained. The story has shown us a simple truth about life. Good plans are wonderful to make. People have often spoken about great ideas. However, strong people are truly hard to find. The mice have accepted their end in the old house.

Time has continued to move forward in the house. The mice have grown older and better. They have shared stories with the young mice. They have told them about the famous meeting. The young mice have listened with wide eyes. They have understood the danger of the black cat. They have also learned about the metal object. They have known that the plan was good. They have realized why nobody placed it on the cat. Fear has kept them all inside the walls. The house has remained a place of constant worry. Life has gone on day after day. The cats have come and gone over the years. New cats have replaced the old ones. The danger has always been present for the little animals. They have always needed to watch their steps. They have never forgotten the hard lesson. Talking has never solved a real problem. Working together has required true strength. Until someone takes the risk, nothing will change. The mice have lived by these words. They have stayed safe inside their dark home. They have faced their fears with quiet strength. The big cat has still lived up. The story has ended right where it began.

### The Fox and the Stork (B1)

Once upon a time, a fast animal and a long bird lived in a large green forest. They lived near a clear blue river. The fast animal was known to be very good. Sometimes he could be quite bad to other animals. One fine morning, the fast animal decided that he would ask the long bird. He wanted her to share a meal at his home. He wanted to play a little game on his friend. He thought it might be funny. The long bird was very happy to receive such a nice message. She arrived at the home right on time. When they sat down at the table, the fast animal served a nice food. He put the food in a flat plate. Because the plate was completely flat, the fast animal could easily eat every drop. He ate all of his food. However, the poor long bird could only touch the end of her long mouth. She touched the surface of the plate. The fast animal laughed quietly. He watched the little long bird struggle with her meal. The good long bird did not show her mind. She thanked the fast animal before she left. A few days later, the long bird decided that she would ask the fast animal. She asked him to dinner by the river. The fast animal happily accepted the message. He was expecting to eat a wonderful meal and laugh again. When the fast animal arrived, dinner was already prepared. It smelled absolutely wonderful. The long bird served the nice meal inside a tall glass bottle. The bottle had a very narrow neck. Because her mouth was long and thin, the long bird could easily reach down. She reached down into the glass bottle. Meanwhile, the fast animal could only touch the outside of the glass. He was unable to get any food. The fast animal realized then that he had lost at his own game. The long bird won the game. He felt very bad and said sorry for his earlier behavior. He understood that people should treat others the way they want to be treated.

The forest was large and full of green trees and cool water. Animals lived together near the clear blue river every single day. The sun was bright upon the tall trees and the green ground. Sometimes the animals played games together in the afternoon. They shared food and sat by the water when the sun went down. Life in the forest was usually quiet. Every animal had a special place to sleep and rest. The river provided cool water for everyone who lived nearby. Birds flew across the sky while fish swam in the deep water. The fast animal and the long bird were part of this big community. They had known each other for a long time. Although they sometimes played games, they usually got along well. The forest remained a safe home for all the animals. Nothing could change the beauty of the green trees and the blue river. Every evening brought a calm silence to the woods. The animals always looked forward to the next morning. They knew that tomorrow would bring new stories and fun times. So they slept quietly under the stars until the sun rose again. Animals would always remember the lessons they learned by the river. Good friends would share meals and respect each other in the woods. Peace and quiet returned to the green trees after the big game. The river kept flowing toward the sea under the bright blue sky.

### Hansel and Gretel (B1)

Once upon a time, two young children named Hansel and Gretel lived near a large forest. The family had very little food, so times were extremely difficult. The parents worried constantly about how they will survive the coming winter. They had no money and almost nothing to eat. Every single day, the father and mother tried to find a solution. However, the problem remained very big and serious. The mother decided that the children must be left deep inside the woods. She believed this choice was the only way to save the family. The father felt sad, but he agreed with her plan. They talked about the idea late into the night. The children listened quietly from their small bed in the corner. They felt very afraid of what might happen next. Hansel thought about a way to keep them safe. He knew the forest well because he played there often. When the sun went down, he gathered small white stones along the path. He put the stones into his pocket and waited for morning. The next day, the parents took Hansel and Gretel deep into the woods. As they walked, Hansel dropped the white stones one by one behind him. The parents left the children alone and walked away quickly. Hansel and Gretel sat down on the ground and waited for hours. When the night sky grew dark, the white stones appeared clear and bright. Those stones helped them find their way back home safely. Their parents looked surprised when the children returned to the house. For a short time, the family had peace again. Unfortunately, the food ran out completely after a few days. The mother made the same harsh plan once more. She told the father that they must leave the children again. This time, Hansel could not gather stones because the door was locked. The next morning, the family walked far into the forest. Hansel used small pieces of food instead of stones. He dropped tiny parts of food along the dark path. Unluckily, small birds flew down and ate every single piece. The birds left Hansel and Gretel completely lost in the deep woods. The brother and sister walked for a long time. They felt tired, cold, and extremely afraid. The wind blew through the trees, making strange noises all around them. They held hands tightly as they moved forward. Suddenly, the brother and sister discovered a strange house. The house was made of sweet food like sugar, bread, and chocolate. They felt very happy because they were extremely empty inside. As they began to eat the walls, an old woman opened the front door. She invited them inside with a big smile. She promised them a wonderful meal and a warm bed for the night. The children entered the room without any fear. They sat at a large table and ate as much as they wanted. After that, the old woman gave them comfortable places to sleep. However, that old woman was actually a bad person who liked to catch children. She wanted to cook them and eat them for dinner. The next morning, she locked Hansel inside a small cage. She made Gretel work hard every single day. Gretel had to clean the floors and bring water from the well. The bad woman planned to cook the boy as soon as he grew fat. Gretel cried every night because she missed her home and parents. She hoped that someone would come and save them. Despite the danger, the young girl remained strong and smart. She watched the bad woman carefully every single day. One morning, the woman told Gretel to check the hot oven. She wanted to bake bread and needed the fire to be very hot. The smart girl pretended that she did not understand. She asked the woman to show her how to check the heat. The bad woman grew angry and leaned inside the hot oven. As soon as the woman was inside, Gretel pushed her quickly. Then she closed the heavy door and locked it tight. The bad woman was completely defeated inside the fire. Gretel ran fast to the small cage and opened the door for Hansel. The brother and sister hugged each other with great joy. They explored the strange house and found a box full of gold coins. Taking the gold with them, Hansel and Gretel ran happily out of the forest. They reached a wide water and saw a friendly bird waiting. The bird carried them across the water safely. Soon, they saw their small house in the distance. Their parents were deeply sorry for what they had done. The family hugged the children and cried tears of joy. With the gold they brought home, they bought plenty of food. The parents never left their children again. Hansel and Gretel grew up healthy and strong. The family lived together in peace for the rest of their lives.
