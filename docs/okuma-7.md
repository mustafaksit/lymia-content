## Basliklar + ozetler

- **How the Leopard Got His Spots** (classic): A worker wants to find a golden creature and makes a plan with a friend.
- **How the Whale Got His Throat** (classic): A giant sea animal follows advice and captures a man who builds a tiny home inside.
- **The Cat That Walked by Himself** (classic): A clever free cat visits a warm new home to find food.
- **How the Rhinoceros Got His Skin** (classic): A hungry rhinoceros eats a man's food and starts a funny change.
- **The Beginning of the Armadillos** (classic): Two small people play near the water, then a man comes with a secret plan.
- **Aladdin and the Wonderful Lamp** (adventure): A poor young boy finds a magic lamp that changes his life forever.
- **Ali Baba and the Forty Thieves** (adventure): Ali Baba finds secret money in a stone cave and faces dangerous thieves.
- **The Voyages of Sinbad (tek sefer)** (adventure): Sinbad joins a crew and sails to a strange island that suddenly moves.

### How the Leopard Got His Spots (B1)

Long ago, a wild cat lived in a hot and dry land among high trees. His skin was yellow like earth and light. He could catch food easily because he looked like dry plants. A hunter also lived in that big area, and he wore brown clothes. The hunter had a fine mind, but catching the animal was a difficult problem. Every day, he tried to find the target, but he always failed. The yellow animal stayed hidden while the sun burned brightly. If the hunter had been faster, he might have caught the individual. After many weeks, the hunter and a good friend changed their plan. They traveled far to a water hole where the cat often went. The good friend told the hunter to change their appearance. They used dark paint to cover themselves from the bright light.

When the cat came down to the water, he saw the two men. The animal was interested because he had never seen such dark figures. He walked closer to look at them while the hunter watched. Suddenly, the hunter reached out and pressed his fingers against the animal skin. Each finger left a dark spot on the yellow body. The cat was surprised and jumped back into the tall plants. He looked down and saw many round marks covering his back. From that day forward, the cat had spots everywhere. The hunter had finally succeeded after working hard for months. The animal learned to be careful near the water hole. Both men returned home with a great story to tell their family.

The land remained hot and dry during the following months. Life continued normally for everyone living in that region. Many days passed after this event. The cat always remembered the lesson from the water hole. He lived safely in the large area for years. The hunter also stayed in the region with his family. They often talked about the day they met the yellow cat. The sun continued to burn brightly over the dry land. Every animal in the area knew about the dark spots. The cat never forgot to check the water hole before drinking. He always looked around very carefully. The friends felt happy about their successful plan.

They never needed to hunt that special cat again. The hot wind blew across the tall plants every afternoon. Nature went on in its usual way. The story of the spotted cat lived on among the people. Generations of children heard the story from their parents. They learned how the cat got his dark marks. The land remained a quiet and beautiful place for all wild animals. Many seasons passed in this quiet part of the world. The sun rose and fell over the green and yellow fields. Every single person remembered the famous hunter and the cat. Life was peaceful and calm for everyone in the region. The great cat walked through the trees every single morning.

### Aladdin and the Wonderful Lamp (B1)

Once upon a time, a young boy lived in a large country with his mother. They had very little money, and life was hard. One day, a strange man came to the house. The man called the boy his lost family member. The man wanted the boy to go with him to a secret place far away. They walked for a long time until they reached a high mountain. The man showed the boy a heavy stone on the ground. He asked him to move it. Under the stone, there was a small room with an old step going down. The man told the boy to bring back a wonderful old lamp. The boy went down and found the object in the dark room. He also saw many beautiful things. However, the man only wanted the lamp. The boy came to the top of the step with the item. The man asked for the item immediately. The boy was afraid. He kept the lamp and said he would give it only after leaving the hole. The angry man pushed the heavy stone back over the hole. He left the boy there by himself. The poor boy sat in the dark and began to cry. He felt so alone. While he was crying, his hand touched the side of the metal object by accident. Suddenly, a huge amazing spirit appeared from the object with a bright light. The powerful spirit told the boy that he would do whatever the master wanted. The boy asked the spirit to help him leave that dark place. He wanted to return home safely. In a single moment, the boy was back at his house with his mother. From that day, the young boy has used the special power to change his life completely.

With the help of the spirit, they have received nice food. They also have got fine clothes and a great new home. The boy grew up into a happy man. People all around the city loved him. He eventually married a wonderful woman. They lived a calm life together. Even though danger tried to return later, the special power always has kept them safe. The boy has never forgotten his past. If the strange man returns, the spirit will protect the family again.

### How the Whale Got His Throat (B1)

Once upon a time, there was a huge animal in the blue sea. This big animal lived in the deep water every single day. He ate all the fish in the water because he was very tired of small food. There was only one small fish left in the huge sea. That little fish was very smart and good at swimming. The smart fish wanted to save his friends from the huge animal. He swam up to the huge animal and asked him a question. The fish asked if the animal had ever eaten a man before. The huge animal replied that he had not eaten any man yet. He wondered what a man actually tasted like. If the animal wants food, he can go near the coast now. The smart fish told the huge animal to swim near the coast. The animal went to the land and found a poor man. That poor man wore blue clothes on his body. The huge animal opened his big mouth wide. He took the poor man and put him inside. The huge animal took the man whole. Inside the animal, the poor man began to jump around. The man made a small house with tree items inside the body. He built a small structure using pieces of tree. This little structure caused the animal a lot of trouble. The huge animal felt very sick inside his body. He tried to throw the man out of his mouth. However, the small structure stayed inside him. Because of this strange experience, the animal felt bad. He has never eaten big things again since that day. Since that day, the huge animal only eats very small fish.

The huge animal learned a very big lesson. If he meets a smart fish, he will listen closely. The smart fish saved all the other small fish. They live happily in the deep blue sea now. The poor man also went back to his home safely. He told his family about the strange experience. No one could believe his funny story at first. The man showed them the pieces of tree. They knew the story was completely true. The huge animal stays far away from the coast now. He swims quietly in the deep blue water. He finds plenty of tiny fish to eat every day. The smart fish watches him from far away. Everything is calm and quiet in the sea again. The animals have a calm life in the water. If anyone asks about the story, the fish will tell them. They remember the day the man built the tree structure. The huge animal will always avoid eating men. That is how the smart fish saved his friends.
