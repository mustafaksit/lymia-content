## Basliklar + ozetler

- **The Open Road** (adventure): Water Rat and Mr Toad take a trip with a horse and a cart.
- **The Tale of Peter Rabbit** (adventure): A naughty rabbit sneaks into a forbidden garden and runs from a scary man.
- **Around the World in Eighty Days** (adventure): Phileas Fogg makes a bold bet to travel around the entire world in eighty days.

- **The Lion and the Mouse** (classic): A tiny mouse promises to help a giant lion after he saves its life.
- **The Boy Who Cried Wolf** (classic): A bored young shepherd lies about a big wolf to trick the townspeople.
- **The Selfish Giant** (classic): A lonely giant builds a wall around his garden, but winter stays forever.
- **The Ugly Duckling** (classic): A very different young child must leave his home and walk far away.

### Around the World in Eighty Days (B1)

Inside a quiet club, the calm Phileas Fogg sat by himself. He made a great bet that he could travel around the world in eighty days. Fogg believed that his plan would succeed. If he works fast, he will win the game. He has made many plans to travel fast. Fogg has known that the world is very big. He has always wanted to prove his great idea. The club members watched him with much interest. They thought about the long journey ahead. Fogg has prepared for the trip with care. He has packed his money and clothes. His friends hoped that he would return safely. Fogg has always been a man of few words. He has never shown any fear about the future. The bet was very important to him. He has decided to leave London at once.

With his new man Passepartout, he left at once by train. They crossed many countries while they faced storms and bad delays. Fogg has kept moving forward without any stop. Passepartout has helped Fogg during every problem on the road. They have traveled through many strange places. The train moved fast across the land. They have seen many different people and towns. Fogg has paid for all tickets without any trouble. They have crossed rivers and hills on their way. Passepartout has watched the time every single hour. They have faced bad weather and long delays. Still, Fogg has kept his calm face. They have run from city to city with great speed. Fogg has never missed a single train. They have moved day and night to win the bet.

A police officer wrongly believed that Fogg was a bank robber. This man followed them closely from city to city. The officer tried to delay their journey at every moment. Despite trouble, Fogg pressed on. The officer has watched every move that Fogg made. He has thought that Fogg took money from a bank. The police have wanted to stop Fogg for a long time. The officer has tried to catch them at each station. Fogg has not known about this danger. He has kept his eyes on the road ahead. The officer has followed them across the land. They have moved from one town to another very quickly. The police man has waited for the right paper to stop them. Fogg has continued his trip despite these bad events. He has not let anything slow him down.

Fogg also helped a kind traveler named Aouda. She joined them, and together they continued across the water. Fogg has kept the group safe during problems. Aouda has thanked Fogg for his great help. They have traveled together across the blue water on a large ship. Aouda has become a good friend to both men. They have shared food and stories on the way. Fogg has protected her from all danger. They have crossed the great water without fear. Aouda has enjoyed the long journey with them. The group has grown closer during the trip. They have faced high waves and wind. Fogg has always stayed strong for the group. Aouda has helped them understand new places. They have moved forward day after day.

Fogg arrived back in London just in time. He won his big bet and proved his journey was possible. The club members have welcomed him back with great joy. Fogg has shown that his plan was right. He has won the money and the game. Aouda and Passepartout have stayed by his side. The police officer has found out the truth at last. Fogg has proved that he was never a bad man. They have lived a happy life after the long trip. Fogg has remembered every moment of the adventure. The whole world has talked about his great journey. He has shown that anything can happen with a good plan. Fogg has finally rested in his quiet home. He has won the greatest bet of his life. The story of his travel has become famous everywhere.

### The Lion and the Mouse (B1)

A big animal was resting in the warm air. While he was sleeping, a tiny mouse ran across his large foot. The tiny mouse woke him up. The angry animal caught the little friend in his heavy hand. The small mouse made a request for freedom. The big animal felt glad by the tiny person and let it go free. The grateful mouse promised that it would return the help one day. The animal only laughed at the idea.

Some time later, the powerful animal was caught fast in a strong network. He tried hard to break free. He could not move no matter how much he worked. His big cry moved across the land while he waited in fear. Hearing the big cry, the little mouse ran over to help him. It used its sharp head to cut through the thick network quickly. The great animal was finally set free from the network by his small friend. This surprising event proved that even the smallest friend can help the strongest. Both animals walked away into the day with glad hearts.

The sun was bright and warm. The day was very long and nice. Every animal in the forest lived in peace. The mouse and the big friend became close. They often met each other in the green plant area. They talked about many things. The mouse told stories about the forest. The big animal listened with much care. Life was good for both of them. They never forgot that day.

Friends can always find a way to help. Small friends are very important. Big friends also need help sometimes. This story shows the power of true love. People should always be kind to others. Good actions always bring good results in the end. The forest remained quiet and safe. Birds sang songs in the tall trees. The wind blew softly through the leaves. Everything was calm.

The animals walked together toward the river. They drank cool water. They played games in the afternoon. Night came slowly over the land. Stars gave light to the dark night. Both friends went to sleep with quiet minds. They knew tomorrow would bring new gladness. The forest was their home. They loved their home very much. No danger could reach them now.

They were safe together. The story of the mouse and the big animal lived on. Others heard the story and learned from it. Good actions are never lost. They come back to us in time. Always help those who need you. You will see the good results. Life is better with good friends. Save every friend you have. They are the true value of life.

The forest kept its quiet secrets. Time passed quietly. Years went by. The two friends stayed together through every season. Winter brought cold weather. They shared food and heat. Spring brought new flowers and bright colors. They ran through the fields with gladness. Summer brought warm days and long sun power. They rested under the cover of big trees.

Every moment was special. They were happy every single day. No problem could ever break their bond. They trusted each other completely. Trust is the base of a strong connection. They knew this fact very well. The forest animals looked up to them. They were an example for everyone. Peace and love ruled the land. The story ends here, but the connection goes on.

### The Selfish Giant (B1)

Once upon a time, a huge man lived in a wonderful house. The house had a large, beautiful garden. Every afternoon, little children came to play in the green land. They listened to sweet birds sing in the trees. The garden was full of lovely flowers. The children always felt very happy there. They loved the sun and the warm air. The birds flew above the nice plants. Every single day brought new joy. The children ran around with big smiles. They played games until the evening came. The man was away in a distant country. No person told the children to stay out. The wonderful garden belonged to the kids. They shared the space and had great fun. The flowers grew tall and bright. The sky stayed blue and clear. Nothing bad happened during those peaceful hours.

One day, the man returned home after a long absence. He was very angry when he saw children playing in his garden without a word. He shouted and told them that the place belonged only to him. To keep everyone out, the man built a high stone wall around the garden. He put up a big sign on the wall. The poor children had no other place to play. They felt very sad about the change. Soon, spring left the land. A long winter of cold ice and white snow covered the garden. The cold wind blew through the trees. Ice covered the ground day after day. The man could not understand why spring never came back. He sat by his window, feeling very cold. He felt sad and had no friends. The white snow stayed all year. The plants died in the cold ice. No birds sang in the bare trees. The man looked out at the dead land. He wished for warm weather to return. The heavy snow covered every single path.

One morning, the children found a small gap in the wall. They slipped back inside the secret garden. The trees grew green leaves again. Lovely spring returned to that part of the garden. Only in one corner, winter remained. A tiny boy could not reach up to the tree branches. He stood under the cold bough and cried. The little child had no way to climb up. The snow and ice stayed on that single branch. The other children tried to help him. They could not reach the high wood. The boy looked up with tears in his eyes. He wanted to sit on the strong limb. The cold wind blew around his small body. He shivered in the freezing air. The winter refused to leave that corner. The other kids watched him with sad faces.

The man looked outside. He finally understood that his own mind had caused the long winter. Filled with deep sorrow, he walked out of his house. He helped the little boy up into the tree. He used his hands to lift the child. The man saw this lovely scene. He decided to tear down the high wall. He took a heavy tool and broke the stone. The wall fell down piece by piece. The children watched the man with wide eyes. They smiled as the stone tumbled to the ground. The man felt a warm feeling in his chest. He knew that he had done a good thing. The sun shone down upon them. The cold ice melted away into water. The small boy hugged the kind man. Spring filled the entire garden at last.

From that day forward, the garden belonged to the children. The man became very kind to everyone. He played with the boys and girls every single afternoon. Surrounded by happy friends and beautiful flowers, the man found true joy. The children laughed and ran across the green land. Birds sang sweet songs in the tall trees. The sun warmed the earth every single morning. No wall stood in the way of the kids. The man sat under the green branches. He watched the little ones play games. Life was good and full of peace. The dark days of winter were gone. The wonderful garden stayed bright and full of life. Every person in the village knew about the kind man. The children visited him day after day. They shared food and stories in the warm sun. The happy man smiled at his young friends. They lived in peace for the rest of their days.
