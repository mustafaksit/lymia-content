## Rastgele 3 tam B1 (veya B2) metni

### The Peddler of Swaffham (B1)

Once upon a time, a simple man lived in a town named Swaffham. His name was John. He worked hard as a person who sold things from house to house. He walked from house to house every day to sell items to people. One night, John had a strange dream while he was sleeping in his bed. A voice told him that a great reward was waiting on a bridge in London. The dream came back to him again and again during many nights. If he goes to the city, he will find a great gift. Finally, the man decided to walk all the way to London. When John arrived at the bridge, he found shops and heavy traffic everywhere. He spent a whole day watching for the promised reward near the water. Nothing happened, and the sun began to go down behind the houses. John felt very tired, and his feet hurt from the long journey. As he rested near a small store, the owner of the store asked him why he was there.

John told the truth about his strange dream to the store owner. The store owner began to laugh out with a big sound. The man told John that bad dreams should never be followed by anyone. The store owner said that he had dreamed of a house in Swaffham too. He said a man named John had gold under his bed in that house. John knew immediately that the house was his own home in the town. He realized that if he checks his bedroom, he will see the money. The happy man thanked the store owner and turned back to his town. When John arrived at his house, he began to look under his bed. Deep in the ground, a massive box of gold was discovered by him. He has always wanted a better life, and now he has it. He has found the gold, and he is very rich today. John will stay in his house and enjoy his life with all the new gold. He will never forget the strange dream that led him to such wealth. The people in the town were very happy for him after that day. John always tells his story to friends who visit his home in Swaffham.

### Snow-White and Rose-Red (B1)

Two young sisters named Snow-White and Rose-Red lived in a small house. They lived near a deep forest. Snow-White was quiet and good. Rose-Red liked to run and play outside. They were good children. They loved their mother very much. Every single day, the sisters helped around the house. They cleaned the floors and prepared nice food for their family. The mother always thanked them for their hard work and support. Life was calm and quiet in the small home near the trees.

During the cold winter months, a big black bear often came to their door. He came to seek rest from the freezing weather outside. At first, the girls were afraid of him. The kind animal showed that he meant no trouble. They let him come inside. He slept near the fire every night. The sisters gave him food and water. They felt safe with their large new friend. The bear stayed warm until the snow began to pass away. He never hurt anyone in the house.

When spring arrived and the weather became warm, the bear had to leave. He went away to protect his gold from a bad man. The sisters were very sad to see their animal friend go into the woods. They had shared many wonderful moments with him during the winter months. If the bear returns, they will welcome him back with open arms. They have missed him every single day since he left. The forest felt empty without his big presence near the door. Spring flowers grew around the house, but the girls still felt sad.

A few weeks later, while they were walking, they found a small man. The man had a long beard. This mean little man was in trouble because his hair was caught in a tree. The two sisters helped him several times. He was never nice to them. He always spoke with a strong voice and made angry sounds. The girls still helped him because they were good children. The small man showed no happiness for their help. He walked away and left them alone in the deep woods.

One day, they saw the same bad man shouting at a large animal. That animal was actually their old friend, the big bear. The bear had finally returned to catch the bad man. The bear hit the mean man hard until he ran away into the forest. Then the bear changed into a rich man because a spell had been broken. The man told them that the bad man had stolen his gold. He had also suffered a spell for a very long time. The sisters were amazed by this big change. They recognized their old friend immediately.

Snow-White later married the happy man. Rose-Red married his brother. They lived together in a huge house near the deep forest. They shared all their gold with their mother. If they see the poor man again, they will help him. They have lived a happy life since the spell ended. Their mother has enjoyed living with them in the big house. The two families have shared many wonderful meals together every week. Everyone in the area knows their story and remembers the big bear.

### The Twelve Dancing Princesses (B1)

There once was a king. He had twelve lovely daughters. They lived in one room together. Every single night, the king locked their door. He locked it securely before he went to rest. However, when morning came, their shoes were always worn through completely. Nobody could understand how that strange thing happened. The door remained locked all night long.

The king made a public statement to the country. He promised a great reward. Any man who solved this problem might choose one daughter to be his wife. Many young men tried to stay up late outside the bedroom door. They always fell fast down in deep sleep. A soldier who had been fighting in wars heard about this challenge. He decided to try his luck.

On his way to the large house, an old woman gave him a special cover. She told him that the cover would make him hidden. She said he would be completely hidden during any time he wore it. When the soldier arrived, the king welcomed him nicely. The king gave him a room near the daughters. At night, the oldest daughter brought him a cup of sweet wine.

The smart soldier did not drink the wine. He acted like he fell fast down in deep sleep instead. Soon, the twelve daughters got up. They put on lovely clothes and laughed quietly. The youngest daughter said that the soldier was sleeping deeply. She said he would never open his eyes. They opened a secret door under their bed. They walked down into the dark earth.

Wearing his special cover, the soldier followed them closely down the hidden steps. At one point, he touched a daughter dress. This made her sister feel quite worried. Soon they arrived at a wonderful forest. All the leaves were made of gold and metal. The soldier broke off a small metal branch to keep as evidence of their journey.

They continued walking until they reached a broad river. Twelve other men waited in boats. Each daughter got into a boat with one man. They crossed the water to a bright house. Inside the bright house, beautiful music played. Everyone danced happily all night long. The daughters danced until their shoes were completely broken by morning.

Before the sun rose, the men brought them back across the river. The daughters ran quickly to their room. The soldier followed them closely. He got back into his bed just before they opened their eyes. When the king questioned him the next morning, the soldier told him everything. He showed the king the metal branch. He explained the secret night dance.

The king called his twelve daughters. He asked them if the soldier was telling the truth. Realizing that their secret plan was completely discovered, the daughters admitted everything. The soldier chose the oldest daughter to be his lovely wife. He became part of the family. From that day on, everyone lived happily together in the great house.
